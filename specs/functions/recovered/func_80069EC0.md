# func_80069EC0

- The USA body spans VRAM `0x80069EC0` through `0x8006A3EB`, ROM `0x6AAC0`,

  `func_8006A3EC` begins next.
- Direct callers are `func_8006A49C` and `func_8006A9D4`. Its vector,

  the matching unit.
- Japan and Europe equivalents begin at `0x8006AA30` and `0x8006F3A0`.

## Behavior

The routine resolves a velocity against a contact normal, normalizes the
remaining tangential direction, and applies an impact-scaled impulse to the
body. It updates steering and collision flags, classifies the hit by its
forward/side direction, and sends a collision message when the impact is
strong enough.
