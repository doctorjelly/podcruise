# func_8008E9F0

## Verified facts

- The USA body spans VRAM `0x8008E9F0` through `0x8008EADB`, ROM `0x8F5F0`,
  236 bytes. The apparent split at `0x8008E9F4` is false because the preceding
  instruction computes its switch index. `jr ra` is at `0x8008EAD4`, followed
  by a `nop` delay slot.
- `func_800887D0` calls it directly, and `func_8008D5FC` registers it as a
  state-handler callback. Its only callees are indirect callbacks loaded from
  attached objects; it references no global.
- Japan and Europe equivalents begin at `0x8008F6E0` and `0x80093F40`.

## Inferences

- The command-7 payload is reinterpreted through a 12-byte stack object. Only
  its first word is observed here, so the remaining bytes have no assigned
  semantics in the recovered type.

## Behavior

Apply a state command: attach an object, initialize or finish activity,
replace a floating-point parameter from the command payload, or set a pending
flag. Commands 4 and 9 notify the attached object with a cleared payload;
unhandled commands forward the original payload. The attached object is read
only by the commands that may notify it, rather than before the switch.

## Status

Byte-matching C. IDO 5.3 `-O2` emits the exact 236-byte USA body, including the
switch jump-table relocation, stack frame, indirect-call delay slots, return,
and final `nop` delay slot.
