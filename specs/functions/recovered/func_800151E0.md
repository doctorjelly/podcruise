# func_800151E0

## Boundary

USA: VRAM 0x800151E0-0x8001523B inclusive, 92 bytes, ROM 0x15DE0.
The body closes with a return whose delay slot is a no-op. The label is
reached by three direct calls in the USA build, and the preceding routine
(func_800151C0) ends with its own return before this entry, so the assigned
boundary is correct. One internal forward branch targets the epilogue; the
branch is of the "likely" form, so a copy of the epilogue's first instruction
sits in its delay slot. That does not extend the function.

## Callers

Three direct call sites in USA (two in the duplicated overlay pair around
0x8006BEA0, one at 0x8007C740). All of them build a two-component vector in
their own stack frame from differences of two other vectors and pass its
address.

## Callees

- func_800151C0 - returns the length of a two-component vector whose address
  is its only argument. It reads both components, squares them, adds the
  squares and takes the square root.

## Globals

- D_800A87F0 - single-precision floating-point constant, read once, 4-byte
  access. It acts as the smallest length for which the routine is willing to
  divide.

All three regional bodies were read independently and are instruction-for-
instruction the same shape as the USA body, differing only in the relocated
call target and constant address.

## Recovered behaviour

The routine normalises a two-component vector in place and reports its
original length.

1. It asks the helper for the length of the vector whose address it was
   given, and remembers that length.
2. If the length is at least as large as the guard constant, it divides both
   components by the length, writing them back into the caller's vector.
   Otherwise the vector is left untouched, which avoids dividing by a value
   too close to zero.
3. It returns the length measured in step 1, whether or not the division was
   performed.

The caller's pointer is reloaded from the incoming argument's home slot after
the helper call, because the helper is free to clobber the argument register.

## Status

Exact in USA, Japan, Europe and lrg_rev1.
