# func_8002C780 recovery note

The current behavior-recovered C remains non-exact under the accepted IDO 5.3
profile: 1192/1192 bytes, 55 differing words, first difference `0x1`.
Two direct callers target `0x8002C780`. Its 12-byte entry prefix loads
`D_8011A240` and leaves its word at offset `0x20` live in `t6`; execution
falls through into the frame-allocating body labeled `func_8002C78C`. These
labels therefore form one callable routine, not two C functions. A
pointer-alias variant for `D_8011A240` increased the mismatch to 74 words and
was reverted. No source change was retained.
