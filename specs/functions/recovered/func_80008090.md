# func_80008090

## Verified facts

- The USA body spans VRAM `0x80008090` through `0x8000852F`, ROM `0x8C90`,
  1184 bytes. `jr ra` is at `0x80008528`; its delay slot restores the 80-byte
  frame, and `func_80008530` begins next.
- `func_800073B4` calls it. It operates on the two eight-entry tables at
  `D_800D1F38` and `D_800D2038`, the audio state globals at `D_8009A2BC`
  through `D_8009A328`, and the timing values `D_80120BE8/D_80120BF0`.
- Its JP and EU equivalents retain the USA address and matched structure.

## Behavior

The routine eases a global audio level toward its target, then synchronizes
eight desired channel records with eight cached records. It expires stale
entries, stops or restarts changed sounds, and updates volume, pitch, flags,
and playback state only where cached values differ.

## Status

Behavior recovered, not byte-matching. IDO 5.3 `-O2` emits the exact 1184-byte
length but first differs at byte `0xC1`; 21 instruction words differ across 15
blocks. The USA build retains assembly.
