## Behavior

Reject an ordinary low-numbered selector without starting a transfer. For an
accepted request, initialize the interface and rebuild the shared command
buffer whenever its mode or requested length changed: clear the requested
prefix, write its fixed header fields, encode the selector, and copy the
32-byte payload. Send the buffer, compute the payload check byte, and wait for
completion. Poll the response, normalize controller error bits to status one,
and invoke the recovery helper on a check-byte mismatch. A successful recovery
requests another poll, with at most three repeats after the initial attempt.
Always shut down the interface before returning the final status.

## Recovery status

The C is independently expressed and behavior-recovered. The USA compiler
comparison is not exact. Rewriting the clear loop with an explicit increment
reproduces the canonical four-byte unroll and its pointer-update delay slot,
reducing the comparison from 26 to 21 differing words. The remaining first
difference is byte `150` (`0x96`) and is register allocation; later differences
include constant scheduling and the final status spill slot. No byte match is
claimed.
