# func_80026B3C

## Verified facts

- The USA body spans VRAM `0x80026B3C` through `0x8002714F`, ROM `0x2773C`,
  1556 bytes. `jr ra` is at `0x80027148`; its delay slot restores the
  192-byte stack frame. `func_80027150` begins next.
- Its direct caller is `func_8004A880`. Its camera, transform, object, audio,
  text, and session helpers and globals are recorded in the matching unit.
- Japan and Europe equivalents begin at `0x800273CC` and `0x800291B0`.

## Behavior

The routine initializes two camera paths and two scene objects for a timed
session transition. It advances the countdown, moves the objects, stops early
on cancel flags, and at two timer windows plays the corresponding cue, swaps
camera endpoints, and draws the appropriate participant message.

## Status

Behavior recovered from the USA binary and independently expressed in C; not
byte-matching. IDO 5.3 `-O2` emits 1564 bytes for the 1556-byte target and first
differs at byte `0x3`; 386 instruction words differ across four broad blocks.
Regional binaries were used only for structural and relocation confirmation.
