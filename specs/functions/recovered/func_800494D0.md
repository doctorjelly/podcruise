- Direct calls occur at `0x80024B50`, `0x80025A94`, and `0x80026854` in the

- Japan and Europe equivalents begin at `0x8004A108` and `0x8004DFF0`.

## Behavior

The routine advances wrapped angles for the course choices and their orbiting
objects. It refreshes shared course state, transforms and scales the selected
course object plus a secondary object, and then positions each configured
orbiting object from its sine/cosine path before submitting its matrix and
restoring visibility state.
