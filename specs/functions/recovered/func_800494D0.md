# func_800494D0

## Verified facts

- The USA body spans VRAM `0x800494D0` through `0x80049C13`, ROM `0x4A0D0`,
  1860 bytes. `jr ra` is at `0x80049C0C`; its delay slot restores the
  208-byte stack frame. `func_80049C14` begins next.
- Direct calls occur at `0x80024B50`, `0x80025A94`, and `0x80026854` in the
  two recovered interface-controller sources. Its object, matrix, rotation,
  scaling, visibility, and trigonometric helpers and globals are recorded in
  the matching unit.
- Japan and Europe equivalents begin at `0x8004A108` and `0x8004DFF0`.

## Behavior

The routine advances wrapped angles for the course choices and their orbiting
objects. It refreshes shared course state, transforms and scales the selected
course object plus a secondary object, and then positions each configured
orbiting object from its sine/cosine path before submitting its matrix and
restoring visibility state.

## Status

Behavior recovered from the USA binary and independently expressed in C; not
byte-matching. IDO 5.3 `-O2` emits the exact 1860-byte length and first differs
at byte `0x23`; 129 instruction words differ across 59 blocks. Regional
binaries were used only for structural and relocation confirmation.
