# func_8004CA50

## Boundary and interface

The canonical USA body occupies ROM `0x4D650` / VRAM `0x8004CA50` through
the stack-restoring return delay slot at `0x8004DC08`, exactly `0x11BC`
(4,540) bytes. `func_8004DC0C` begins immediately afterwards. The Japan and
Europe structural counterparts begin at `0x8004D6BC` and `0x80051570`; their
different sizes make them relocation and control-flow evidence only.

Two recovered callers pass one context pointer. The body reads or updates its
mode words at offsets `0x08`, `0x0C` and `0x10`, selected track/object words at
`0x34` and `0x40`, stage words at `0x38` and `0x3C`, signed option bytes at
`0x50` and `0x6C`, and a signed count at `0x70`.

The body directly calls the display-slot, vector, matrix, angle, random-number,
scene-object, camera, track and lighting helpers represented in the matching
unit. It uses the track records at `D_800A4C00`, the position tables from
`D_800A5080` through `D_800A51B4`, the camera matrices beginning at
`D_80118D20`, and the transition block at `D_8011A240`.

## Behavior

Reset shared transition fields, establish two identity-like camera transforms,
enable the rendering control bit, and disable the currently counted rider
objects. Default the selected track and object fields. Entering stage three
from another stage also creates a fixed pair of scene objects plus a randomly
chosen, non-repeating member of a three-entry position set.

Then configure scene objects according to the context mode:

- modes 9, 12 and 13 enable transition flags and conditionally select track
  `0x25` from the previous mode;
- mode 8 selects track `0x14` and transition mode 7;
- mode 7 chooses a track-side option, places the principal object, chooses a
  random secondary object, initializes each rider object, and arranges the
  remaining auxiliary objects around an arc;
- mode 4 finds the first track record of kind four, places rider and principal
  objects relative to it, clears unused auxiliaries, and conditionally creates
  one of two named special objects;
- mode 18 chooses the order of two objects from the side byte, blends their
  position, and initializes both with the state-dependent setup mode;
- modes 15 and 16 create short fixed formations; mode 17 creates one distant
  object and selects track `0x1A`.

Finally configure the main viewport and display slot, attach the three camera
matrices, set the view parameters using the mode-selected far plane, copy the
chosen track vectors into the camera transforms, derive three orientation
records, and publish the default light unless stage three owns its lighting.

## Status

Behavior recovered from the canonical USA body and independently expressed in
C. IDO 5.3 `-O2 -mips2` emits 4,408 bytes for the 4,540-byte target and first
differs at byte `0x3`; its stack frame and register/local allocation differ
throughout the large switch. This is not byte-matching and is not eligible for
hybrid-build substitution.
