# func_8004850C

## Verified facts

- The USA body spans VRAM `0x8004850C` through `0x80048A4F`, ROM `0x4910C`,
  1348 bytes. `jr ra` is at `0x80048A48`; its delay slot restores the
  328-byte stack frame. `func_80048A50` begins next.
- Direct callers are `func_8001F5AC`, `func_80024070`, and `func_8004A880`.
  Its vector, matrix, ray-query, transform, and object helpers and globals are
  recorded in the matching unit.
- Japan and Europe equivalents begin at `0x80049144` and `0x8004D02C`.

## Behavior

The routine updates several scene-object transforms. For each enabled entry it
builds a transform at a ray hit with axes aligned to the hit normal, applies
selection-specific rotations and scaling, and submits the result to the
corresponding object. A second path derives another object's transform from the
shared camera/reference vectors and current selection state.

## Status

Behavior recovered from the USA binary and independently expressed in C; not
byte-matching. IDO 5.3 `-O2` emits 1340 bytes for the 1348-byte target and first
differs at byte `0x3`; 143 instruction words differ across 36 blocks. The local
matrix scratch buffer is 16 floats because `func_800156DC` copies all four
four-float rows; the host CI diagnostic caught and corrected the earlier
undersized declaration.
