# func_80077C94

## Boundary and regional evidence

The USA body is VRAM `0x80077C94` through `0x800784F8`, ROM `0x078894`,
2,148 bytes. It starts with a 216-byte stack frame and ends with `jr $ra` at
`0x800784F0`; the delay slot is `nop`. The two direct USA calls are at
`0x800792C8` and `0x80079300` inside `func_800784F8`.

The unique structural equivalents are Japan `0x800788C4` / ROM `0x0794C4`
and Europe `0x8007D174` / ROM `0x07DD74`. Their common relocation pattern
supports the USA boundary; USA remains canonical.

## Behavior

Reject a missing or disqualified craft and any craft outside the usable fade
range. Read flags from its optional attached item, build a reordered and
normalized transform from the supplied matrix, and derive a distance fade.
Choose color and intensity values from the global environment mode and item
flags, apply the global channel scales, and submit the result through
`func_8007B34C`. If that submission and the remaining thresholds succeed,
emit the final effect request through `func_80008B14`.

The original loads the local word passed as the final `func_8007B34C`
argument before ever writing it when `arg6` is zero. When `arg6` is nonzero it
ORs bit zero into that same word. The C intentionally preserves this
indeterminate-stack behavior rather than inventing a zero initialization.

## Status

Behavior recovered from the USA binary and independently expressed in C; not
byte-matching. IDO 5.3 `-O2` emits 2,100 bytes for the 2,148-byte target and
first differs at byte `0x3`. Regional binaries were used only for structural
and relocation confirmation.
