# func_800695D4

## Verified facts

- The USA body spans VRAM `0x800695D4` through `0x80069A63`, ROM `0x6A1D4`,
  1168 bytes. `jr ra` is at `0x80069A5C`; its delay slot restores the 160-byte
  frame, and `func_80069A64` begins next.
- `func_8006A49C` calls it. The body uses the vector helpers from
  `func_80014D4C` through `func_800155EC`, constants `D_800AD540` through
  `D_800AD560`, `D_8011C854`, and frame time `D_80120BF0`.
- JP and EU equivalents begin at `0x8006A144` and `0x8006EAB4`.

## Behavior

The routine projects an object's orientation against an input direction. For
out-of-range alignment it clears the outputs and damps stored motion. Otherwise
it derives a perpendicular response, limits and integrates that response,
updates the stored vector, and computes a signed steering/traction value from
horizontal direction and speed.

## Status

Behavior recovered, not byte-matching. IDO 5.3 `-O2` emits 1160 bytes against
the 1168-byte target and first differs at byte `0x6E`. The USA build retains
assembly.
