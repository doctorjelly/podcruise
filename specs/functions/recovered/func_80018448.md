- The body is a return whose delay slot is empty; the two instructions are the

  - the immediately preceding routine at `0x80018440` is itself a complete

`0x80018448`, `0x80018450`, `0x80018460`), each separately called; being small
and adjacent is normal here and is not evidence of a bad boundary.

## Recovered behaviour

The routine does nothing and returns immediately. It reads no memory, writes no
memory, takes no arguments and produces no result. It survives in the shipped
image because it is still called from the routine at `0x8002F794`, presumably a
hook whose body was removed or was never filled in for the retail build.
