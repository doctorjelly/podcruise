# func_80029298

## Boundary

- USA VRAM `0x80029298` through `0x8002932B`, ROM `0x29E98`, 148 bytes.
- The indirect jump at `0x800292E0` has an empty delay slot. All jump-table
  arms rejoin the loop at `0x80029318`.
- The function returns at `0x80029324` with an empty delay slot. The next
  independent body begins at `0x8002932C`.

## References

Direct calls occur at `0x8001AE10` and `0x8001C1EC`; both supply a floating
argument in `$f12`. The function is a leaf. It walks the 32-byte records from
`D_800A4C00` up to `D_800A5100` and uses a six-entry local jump table at
`0x800A9DF0`. Japan and Europe preserve structurally equivalent bodies, but
USA is the canonical recovery target.

## Behavior

For each of 40 records, the signed halfword at offset `0x18` selects an update
through its value plus one. Original phase `-1` or `4` writes `-145.0f` plus
the argument at offset `0x08`; phase `0` writes `-60.0f` plus the argument;
phase `1` writes `-157.0f` plus the argument at both offsets `0x08` and `0x14`;
and phase `2` writes that same value only at `0x08`. Other phases leave the
record unchanged. The phase itself is only read, not incremented or stored.

## Status

Behavior recovered, not byte-matching. IDO 5.3 emits the correct 148-byte
extent, jump table, constants, cases, loop shape, and delay slots. It assigns
the current and end pointers to `$v0` and `$v1` in the opposite order from the
original, with the first byte difference at offset `0x15`; no exact claim is
made.
