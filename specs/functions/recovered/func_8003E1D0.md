# func_8003E1D0

## Boundary

- USA VRAM range `0x8003E1D0`..`0x8003E1EB` (inclusive), ROM `0x3EDD0`, size 28
  bytes. Matches the assignment.
- The body is three stores; the third occupies the delay slot of the return.

## Callers

Two direct call sites in the USA build.

## Callees

None. Leaf function.

## Globals and access widths

Three 32-bit variables, each written as a full word of zero:

- `D_800A4970`
- `D_800A4978`
- `D_800A4984`

Elsewhere in the same translation unit each is read as a word and two of them
have their address taken, so they are the heads of small records rather than
isolated scalars.

## Arguments

None. Returns nothing.

## Recovered behavior

This is the reset entry point for a small subsystem that keeps three independent
slots of state (two queue-like records whose addresses are handed out elsewhere,
plus one counter or active-handle word). Calling it clears the counter word and
both slot heads to zero, abandoning whatever was queued without freeing or
notifying anything.

The store order the original compiler produced (counter word first, then the two
slot heads in address order) follows directly from writing the three assignments
in that order in the source.

| europe | 0x80042B10 | 0x43710 | 0x800A9C70 / 9C78 / 9C84 |
| lrg_rev1 | 0x8003E1D0 | 0x3EDD0 | same as usa |

Each regional body was read independently; the three variables keep the same
relative spacing (0, +8, +20) and the same store order in every version.

## Status

Exact. Byte-identical in USA, Japan, Europe and lrg_rev1.
