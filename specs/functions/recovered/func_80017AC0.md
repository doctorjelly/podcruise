# func_80017AC0

## Boundary
- USA VRAM 0x80017AC0 .. 0x80017B33 (116 bytes), ROM 0x186C0.
- Terminates with the return jump at 0x80017B2C and an empty delay slot at
  0x80017B30. Assigned boundary confirmed correct.
- One direct caller, which targets 0x80017AC0; no stray label or shared tail.

## Callers
- One call site (USA 0x80008C84) inside a larger routine. It passes three
  pointers into its own frame: a 16-byte destination, a 12-byte source and a
  12-byte reference vector.

## Callees
- func_800154D0 (USA 0x800154D0). One argument, a pointer to three floats; it
  measures the vector's length and, when the length is at least a global
  threshold, divides the three components in place. It returns the length,
  which this routine ignores.

## Globals
- None referenced directly.

apart from the relocated call target.

## Recovered behaviour
Constructs a plane-like four-float record from a direction and a point.

1. Copies the three floats of the source vector into the first three slots of
   the destination.
2. Normalises those three slots in place through the shared length/normalise
   helper.
3. Writes into the fourth slot the dot product of the now-normalised triple with
   the three floats of the reference vector.

Access width is 32-bit float throughout; the destination must therefore be at
least four floats wide while the two inputs need only three.

## Source shape notes
- The copy is three separate float element assignments, not a whole-object
  assignment: a block move of a 12-byte object would go through the assembler
  temporary with integer loads and stores, whereas the original moves each
  component through a floating-point register.
- The dot product is written as the plain left-to-right sum
  `d[0]*r[0] + d[1]*r[1] + d[2]*r[2]`; the compiler commutes the final addition
  itself, so no hand-written grouping is needed.
- The destination and the reference pointer are both spilled to their own
  incoming home slots because they have to survive the normalise call; the
  source pointer is dead after the copy and is not spilled. That asymmetry is
  the arity evidence for exactly three parameters.

## Status
Exact in USA on the first attempt, and exact in Japan, Europe and lrg_rev1.
