## Recovered behavior

For each of the four columns of the matrix the routine forms the dot product of
the first three entries of that column with the three components of the point,
adds the fourth entry of the column, and stores the result into the
corresponding slot of the four-element output. That is the standard affine
transform of a point by a row-major matrix whose last row holds the
translation, producing all four components including the homogeneous one. The
three products are summed left to right and the translation term is added last,
which is what the original arithmetic ordering requires.
