# func_80019BB4

## USA source status

The behavior-recovered C draws a startup/menu overlay and updates its
fade and selection state. Its USA ROM body is 2,132 bytes at VRAM
`0x80019BB4`; the linked IDO 5.3 body is 2,120 bytes, with 305 differing
overlap words and three missing words. The USA split remains assembly.

A bounded audit tested shared `mode == 0/1` tails (2,112 bytes, 304
differing words) and a signed-16-bit `mode` local (2,128 bytes, 332
differing words). Both were nonmatching and restored. The remaining
differences are broad, not a verified local register or stack-only fix.
