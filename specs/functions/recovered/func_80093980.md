# func_80093980

## Boundary

VRAM 0x80093980..0x8009398B (12 bytes, USA ROM 0x94580). Three instructions:
an address setup, the return, and the store in the return's delay slot. The
word after the body is padding. Boundary as assigned; no correction.

## Callers

Two direct call sites in USA, at 0x8008B04C and 0x8008B174.

## Callees

None. Leaf function.

## Globals and access widths

Writes one 32-bit word to the memory-mapped signal-processor status register at
physical 0x04040010 through the uncached window at 0xA4040010. As with the
matching reader, the address must be a literal in the source so that its low
half folds into the store displacement.

## Regional addresses

- Japan: 0x80094670, ROM 0x095270
- Europe: 0x80098ED0, ROM 0x099AD0
- lrg_rev1: 0x80093980, ROM 0x94580 (same as USA)

All regional bodies were read independently and are word-for-word identical to
the USA body.

## Recovered behavior

Takes one 32-bit value and writes it straight to the signal-processor status
register. The status register is write-one-to-act, so callers use this to set
and clear the individual status bits; the observed call sites pass a bit mask
constant. Nothing is returned.

## Status

Exact in USA, Japan, Europe and lrg_rev1, at the accepted optimization-level-2
profile and also at the level-1 profile used by the rest of this translation
unit.
