# func_8008A750

## Verified facts

- The callable USA body starts at VRAM `0x8008A750`, ROM `0x8B350`. Branches
  cross three false scanner boundaries and prove that the complete body ends at
  `0x8008A8B8`, for a total of 360 bytes. Its return paths have `cvt.s.d`,
  `neg.s`, a constant load, or `nop` in their delay slots as appropriate.
- Its only direct caller, `func_80014CC0`, passes one float and consumes one
  float result. The routine is a leaf.
- `D_800ADDD0` supplies polynomial coefficients 1 through 4;
  `D_800ADDF8`, `D_800ADE00`, and `D_800ADE08` are the reciprocal and split
  halves of pi. `D_800ADE10` is zero and `D_800AE110` is a NaN.
- The relocated entries are Japan `0x8008B440` (ROM `0x8C040`) and Europe
  `0x8008FCA0` (ROM `0x908A0`). Both have the same 360-byte instruction and
  control-flow layout; only constant addresses move.

## Behavior

Scale the magnitude of a finite input by the reciprocal of pi, offset it by a
half, and round it to select a period. Then reduce by `count - 0.5` multiples
of pi and evaluate an odd polynomial on the reduced value. Negating alternate
periods produces a cosine result, which is even in its input. Inputs whose
single-precision magnitude class is too large return zero; NaNs return the
stored NaN.

## Status

Exact in the canonical USA build: IDO 5.3 `-O2` emits all 360 bytes
identically. Japan and Europe are structural evidence only in this tranche;
their compiler outputs have not been claimed as exact matches.
