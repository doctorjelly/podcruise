## Recovered behaviour

The routine scans the three-slot history in order and returns one as soon as an
entry sign-extends to the argument value, without examining the remaining
entries. If none of the three matches it returns zero. Nothing is written.

Together with `func_80009278` this forms a small "have I seen this recently?"
check over the last three recorded values.
