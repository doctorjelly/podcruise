# `func_800366DC`

## Boundary

- USA VRAM `0x800366DC`, ROM `0x0372DC`, 832 bytes. An earlier assignment
  started eight bytes late; the recovered entry includes the global-mode test.
- The body returns at `0x80036A14`; the `nop` at `0x80036A18` is its delay
  slot. The next address, `0x80036A1C`, is outside the function.
- Japan relocates the equivalent body to `0x8003713C`; Europe relocates it to
  `0x8003B04C`. They were used only as structural evidence.

## Connections

There are four USA call sites, in `func_80036F98` and `func_800374C4`. The
function calls `func_80034374`, `func_800154D0`, and `func_80015538`. It reads
`D_800A3FE8`, `D_800AAAE0`, `D_800AAAE4`, `D_800AAAE8`, and the view pointer
`D_80112C94`; it advances the display-list cursor `D_80112C90`.

## Behavior

When the display-list mode is enabled, fetch the current transform and form a
three-component displacement from its translation to the active view. Choose
two direction vectors from fixed orthogonal pairs when the displacement is
near the view or lies beyond either vertical threshold. Otherwise normalize a
horizontal perpendicular and derive the second direction from its cross
product with the displacement. Scale each of the six direction components by
128, cap positive values at 127, and write them into two light records. Clear
both color copies of the first light, give the second light green intensity
128 in both copies, and append two display-list commands pointing at the two
records.

## Recovery status

The C is independently expressed and behavior-recovered. USA IDO 5.3 `-O2`
produces the correct 832-byte body, stack layout, branches, calls, floating
point operations, stores, return, and delay slot, but it is not byte-matching.
The first difference is byte `392` (`0x188`), and 24 words differ. They are
temporary-register assignments plus one scheduling choice: the original
hoists the first display command's high half while the recovered source hoists
the second light's address. Direct expressions, scalar declaration order,
grouped and split stores, reversed command-field stores, and explicit cursor
updates were compared and rejected. No exact match is claimed.
