# func_80047DB0

## Verified facts

- The USA body spans VRAM `0x80047DB0` through `0x8004850B`, ROM `0x489B0`,
  1884 bytes. `jr ra` is at `0x80048504`; its delay slot restores the
  312-byte stack frame. `func_8004850C` begins next.
- Its only direct caller is `func_8004AE74`. Its marker, gauge, matrix,
  visibility, rotation, scaling, and object-update helpers and globals are
  recorded in the matching unit.
- Japan and Europe equivalents begin at `0x800489E8` and `0x8004C8D0`.

## Behavior

The routine derives a clamped interface alpha, lays out paired status markers,
highlights the selected and final entries, and draws gauges between them. It
then transforms each available scene object from its stored record, applies
selection-specific rotations and final-entry scaling, submits the result, and
restores the object's visibility state.

## Status

Behavior recovered from the USA binary and independently expressed in C; not
byte-matching. IDO 5.3 `-O2` emits 1912 bytes for the 1884-byte target and first
differs at byte `0x3`; 462 instruction words differ across 15 blocks. Regional
binaries were used only for structural and relocation confirmation.
