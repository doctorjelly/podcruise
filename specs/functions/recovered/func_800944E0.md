# func_800944E0

## Verified facts

- The USA body spans VRAM `0x800944E0` through `0x800945BF`, ROM `0x950E0`,
  224 bytes. `jr ra` is at `0x800945B8`; its delay slot restores the 40-byte
  stack frame.
- `func_8008BDC0` installs it as a hardware-transfer callback. Its only direct
  callee is `func_80088360`; it reads the cartridge base at `D_80000308` and
  accesses the PI registers at `0xA4600000` through `0xA4600010`.
- Japan and Europe equivalents begin at `0x800951D0` and `0x80099A30`.

## Behavior

Wait until the peripheral interface is idle, publish physical DRAM and masked
cartridge addresses, and start a read or write using a length-minus-one value.
Directions other than zero or one are rejected with `-1` after the addresses
have been programmed.

## Status

Behavior recovered from the USA binary and independently expressed in C; not
byte-matching. IDO 5.3 `-O2` emits 192 bytes for the 224-byte target and first
differs at byte `0x3`.
