# func_80041258

## Verified facts

- The USA body spans VRAM `0x80041258` through `0x800419B3`, ROM `0x41E58`,
  1884 bytes. `jr ra` is at `0x800419AC` with a nop delay slot;
  `func_800419B4` begins next.
- Its direct callers are `func_800419BC` and `func_80043598`. Its vector,
  matrix, interpolation, trigonometric, rider-state, and completion helpers and
  globals are recorded in the matching unit.
- Japan and Europe equivalents begin at `0x80041A60` and `0x80045BF8`.

## Behavior

When a rider is attached, the routine derives four pairs of camera origins and
targets from the rider and its selected anchor. It advances through three timed
segments, applies sine easing between adjacent pairs, derives the interpolated
heading and pitch, and writes camera and target matrices into the frame. After
the final segment it signals completion.

## Status

Behavior recovered from the USA binary and independently expressed in C; not
byte-matching. IDO 5.3 `-O2` emits 1812 bytes for the 1884-byte target and
differs at the first instruction; 464 instruction words differ across six
broad blocks. Regional binaries were used only for structural confirmation.
