# func_8003ED74

## Boundary

- USA VRAM range: 0x8003ED74 .. 0x8003EDD3 inclusive (0x60 = 96 bytes, 24 words).
- ROM offset 0x3F974.
- The body ends with a return whose delay slot is empty.
- The assignment's size (96) was confirmed correct; no boundary correction. The
  body has no interior labels and both direct call sites target 0x8003ED74.

## Callers

Two direct call sites, both inside func_800870D0. At each of them the first
argument is the literal 4, the second and third are small literal screen
coordinates, and the fourth is the address of a stack buffer that the caller has
just filled with func_8008A6B4.

## Callees

func_8003EA84, taking nine arguments: four in registers and five more on the
outgoing stack area. Its own body shows that its first two arguments are signed
16-bit, its next four are unsigned 8-bit, and the last three are 32-bit.

## Recovered behavior

A convenience wrapper that queues one fully opaque, fully white item.

It calls func_8003EA84 with the two 16-bit coordinates it was given, follows them
with four separate 255 values (the four 8-bit channel arguments, evidently red,
green, blue and alpha, all at maximum), then the caller's pointer argument, then
the caller's selector argument, and finally a literal zero as the last argument.
Nothing is returned.

The two known call sites make this a "draw this text at these coordinates in
solid white" helper.

## Source shape notes

A single expression statement is enough; the compiler materialises each of the
four 255 constants separately rather than sharing one register, and homes the
first and fourth parameters before reloading them into the outgoing stack slots,
which is its normal handling for arguments that are forwarded past the fourth
position.

## Status

Exact on us, jp, eu and lrg_rev1.
