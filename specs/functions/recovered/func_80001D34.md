# func_80001D34

## Boundary

- USA VRAM range: 0x80001D34 .. 0x80001E7C inclusive (0x14C = 332 bytes, 83 instructions).
- USA ROM offset: 0x2934.
- Ends with a return whose delay slot releases the sixteen-byte frame. The frame
  exists solely to preserve one callee-saved double register that holds the
  widened divisor across the two range tests.
- Every early exit branches to that single return. Assigned size 332 was
  correct; no boundary correction.

## Callers and callees

- One direct call site in the USA build. Three pointer arguments and a float
  result.
- Leaf function; no callees.

## Globals and access widths

- `D_800A8128` — one 64-bit float, read once. Lower bound of the rejection band.
- `D_800A8130` — one 64-bit float, read once. Upper bound of the rejection band.

Argument layout deduced from the accesses:

- argument 1: four floats at offsets 0x00..0x0C — a plane, three normal
  components followed by the plane constant.
- argument 2: seven floats at offsets 0x00..0x18 — a ray: origin, direction and
  a maximum travel distance.
- argument 3: three floats at offsets 0x00..0x08 — the output point.

## Recovered behavior

Intersects a ray with a plane and reports how far along the ray the hit lies.

It forms two three-component dot products of the plane normal: one against the
ray origin and one against the ray direction. The second of these is widened to
double precision and tested against the two global bounds; if it lies inside
that band the ray runs too nearly parallel to the plane and the routine gives up
immediately, returning minus one and leaving the output point untouched.

Otherwise it divides the signed distance from the ray origin to the plane by the
direction term to obtain the parameter along the ray. A negative parameter means
the plane lies behind the origin, and a parameter beyond the ray's own maximum
travel means the hit is out of range; either case returns minus one without
writing the output.

For a hit it writes the direction scaled by the parameter into the three output
floats, then adds the ray origin to each of them in place, and returns the
parameter.

Source-shape notes established by matching:

- The two range tests are a single short-circuiting conjunction; each test is a
  double-precision comparison against the widened direction term, and the widened
  value is computed once and kept alive across both tests.
- The output point is genuinely built in two passes over memory: the three
  scaled direction components are stored first, then each slot is re-read and
  advanced by the corresponding origin component. Fusing the two passes into one
  expression per component, or interleaving them component by component, both
  reorder the loads and lose the match.
- The second pass is written as a compound addition onto the output slot.
  Spelling it out as an explicit reload plus assignment schedules the reload of
  the first output slot one instruction later and loses the match.

## Status

Exact. Byte-identical in USA, Japan, Europe and the large-cartridge revision.
