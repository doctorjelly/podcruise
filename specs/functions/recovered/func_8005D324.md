# func_8005D324

## Verified facts

- The USA body spans VRAM `0x8005D324` through `0x8005DBAB`, ROM `0x5DF24`,
  2184 bytes. `jr ra` is at `0x8005DBA4`; its delay slot restores the
  112-byte stack frame. `func_8005DBAC` begins next.
- Its only direct caller is `func_8005DBAC`. Its resource, palette, position,
  scale, color, timing, and effect-command helpers and globals are recorded in
  the matching unit.
- Japan and Europe equivalents begin at `0x8005DECC` and `0x80062808`.

## Behavior

The routine configures a family of interface or particle effects from two mode
arguments. It loads the needed resources, selects position, scale, color,
opacity, and timing values, emits the primary effect, conditionally prepares a
secondary range for two modes, and finishes by configuring the paired effect
IDs and their shared controller.
