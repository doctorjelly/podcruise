  recorded in the matching unit.
- Japan and Europe equivalents begin at `0x8005ED0C` and `0x80063688`.

## Behavior

The routine derives a stage result from two input fields and returns early when
that combination is invalid. Otherwise it loads the associated resource and
target, finds the matching entry in the supplied stage list, initializes its
movement, and chooses timing, sound, color, and effect settings from the stage
category before updating linkage and global effect flags.
