# func_8008DD80

## Verified facts

- The USA body spans VRAM `0x8008DD80` through `0x8008DE9F`, ROM `0x8E980`,
  288 bytes. `jr ra` is at `0x8008DE98`; its delay slot restores the 72-byte
  stack frame.
- `func_8008D554` registers it as an emitter callback. It has no direct-address
  callees or globals: every per-entry emitter is loaded from offset 4 of that
  entry.
- Japan and Europe equivalents begin at `0x8008EA70` and `0x800932D0`.

## Behavior

Begin an output command stream with paired width records. For every entry in
the context, invoke its emitter with the current stream cursor, append six
fixed command words parameterized by twice the width, and return the cursor
immediately following the final record.

## Status

Behavior recovered from the USA binary and independently expressed in C; not
byte-matching. IDO 5.3 `-O2` emits 296 bytes for the 288-byte target and first
differs at byte `0x4`.
