# func_80011F38

## Verified facts

- The USA body spans VRAM `0x80011F38` through `0x800125E3`, ROM `0x12B38`,
  1708 bytes. `jr ra` is at `0x800125DC`; its delay slot stores the final
  display-list word at `0x800125E0`. `func_800125E4` begins next.
- `func_80012BF0` and `func_80014568` contain 42 direct call sites. The routine
  has no callees; its screen bounds, dimensions, and display-list cursor are
  recorded in the matching unit.
- Japan and Europe equivalents begin at `0x80011F80` and `0x800123C8`.

## Behavior

The routine scales a requested rectangle to the active screen dimensions,
expands the global accumulated bounds, and appends three display-list commands.
Those commands draw the textured rectangle, set its texture coordinates, and
set the horizontal and vertical texture steps for the current scale.

## Status

Behavior recovered from the USA binary and independently expressed in C; not
byte-matching. IDO 5.3 `-O2` emits the exact 1708-byte length and first differs
at byte `0x9`; 251 instruction words differ across 70 blocks. Regional binaries
were used only for structural and relocation confirmation.
