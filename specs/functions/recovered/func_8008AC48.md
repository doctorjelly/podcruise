# func_8008AC48 — 64-bit multiply helper

## Boundary
- USA VRAM `0x8008AC48`..`0x8008AC77` (48 bytes), ROM `0x8B848`.
- Ends with `jr $ra` plus a delay-slot instruction producing the upper half of
  the product. No internal labels, no branch from the preceding function
  reaches into the range. Assigned boundary correct.

## Callers / callees
- Ten direct call sites in six distinct callers: `func_8002E134`,
  `func_8002E4B4`, `func_8008126C`, `func_80081368` and neighbours.
- No callees; leaf function with no stack frame beyond the argument home slots.

## Globals
- None.

## Regional addresses
- Japan `0x8008B938` / ROM `0x08C538`.
- Europe `0x80090198` / ROM `0x090D98`.
- lrg_rev1 shares the USA address `0x8008AC48` / ROM `0x8B848`.
- All three regional bodies were read independently and are identical to the
  USA body.

## Recovered behavior
Takes two 64-bit signed integers and returns their product, truncated to 64
bits. Only the low 64 bits of the full 128-bit product are kept, which is why
the signedness of the multiply instruction the compiler chose is not observable
in the result. The argument reassembly and the splitting of the result into an
upper and a lower half are, again, entirely compiler-generated from a plain
`*` on `long long`.

## Build note
Same as `func_8008AB84`: needs the `-mips3 -o32` compile.

## Status
Exact in USA, Japan, Europe and lrg_rev1.
