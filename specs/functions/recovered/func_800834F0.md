# func_800834F0

## Verified facts

- The USA body spans VRAM `0x800834F0` through `0x80083D7F`, ROM `0x840F0`,
  2192 bytes. `jr ra` is at `0x80083D78`; its delay slot restores the
  312-byte stack frame. `func_80083D80` begins next.
- `func_80045694`, `func_80018480`, and `func_800784F8` contain eight direct
  call sites; the routine also calls itself recursively. Its trigonometric and
  child-node helpers, vertex/command templates, sine table, and constants are
  recorded in the matching unit.
- Japan and Europe equivalents begin at `0x800841D0` and `0x800889D0`.

## Behavior

The routine lazily fills a 64-entry sine table and recursively visits flagged
child nodes. For the supported node kind it derives a visibility or amplitude
factor: below a threshold it emits a fixed six-vertex mesh, while larger values
produce an eight-segment deformed strip with sine offsets, interpolation, mesh
triangles, and a terminating display command.

## Status

Behavior recovered from the USA binary and independently expressed in C; not
byte-matching. IDO 5.3 `-O2` emits 2708 bytes for the 2192-byte target and first
differs at byte `0x2`; 673 instruction words differ across two broad blocks.
Regional binaries were used only for structural and relocation confirmation.
