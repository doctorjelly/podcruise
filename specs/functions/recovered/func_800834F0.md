# func_800834F0

## Verified facts

- The USA body spans VRAM `0x800834F0` through `0x80083D7F`, ROM `0x840F0`,
  2192 bytes. `jr ra` is at `0x80083D78`; its delay slot restores the
  312-byte stack frame. `func_80083D80` begins next.
- `func_80045694`, `func_80018480`, and `func_800784F8` contain eight direct
  call sites; the routine also calls itself recursively. Its trigonometric and
  child-node helpers, vertex/command templates, sine table, and constants are
  recorded in the matching unit.
- Japan and Europe equivalents begin at `0x800841D0` and `0x800889D0`.

## Behavior

The routine lazily fills a 64-entry sine table and recursively visits flagged
child nodes. For the supported node kind it derives a visibility or amplitude
factor: below a threshold it emits a fixed six-vertex mesh, while larger values
produce an eight-segment deformed strip with sine offsets, interpolation, mesh
triangles, and a terminating display command.

## Status

Behavior recovered from the USA binary and independently expressed in C; not
byte-matching. After the USA threshold and vertex-layout corrections, IDO 5.3
`-O2` emits 2096 bytes for the 2192-byte target, with 519 differing overlap
words, 24 missing words, and first difference at byte `0x2`.

## USA vertex alignment correction

The 16-byte vertex record contains six halfwords followed by one packed RGBA
word at offset `0xC`. Canonical `main_840F0.s` copies each record with aligned
word operations: `8008369C` through `800836C8` copy offsets 0/4/8/12, and
`80083814`/`80083818` explicitly load/store the final word. The six templates
at `800A6808` through `800A6858` contain `808080FF` or `000000FF` in that
word. This function never accesses its individual color bytes.

Representing that final word as `u32 color` preserves every field offset and
the 16-byte stride while giving the record its evidenced word alignment. The
former four separate byte fields left the type aligned to two bytes, causing
IDO to emit 76 each of `lwl`, `lwr`, `swl`, and `swr`; the canonical body has
none. The corrected source also has none and shrinks from 2704 to 2096 bytes.
A second private type trial used a union containing the same fields and two
`u64` words, giving eight-byte alignment. Its emitted text was byte-identical
to the retained four-byte-aligned record. The remaining loop and allocation
differences are unresolved. No source loop or command emission changed.

The second and third parameters are vector pointers, forwarded unchanged by
the recursive branch. The canonical caller at `80079608`/`8007960C` forms
stack addresses for these arguments before the call at `80079614`; its
second call and the callers in `func_80018480` and `func_80045694` likewise
pass zero vectors. Correcting their former `s32` declarations to `f32 *`
leaves the entire emitted text unchanged.
