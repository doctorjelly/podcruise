# func_80029D38

## Verified facts

- The USA body spans VRAM `0x80029D38` through `0x8002A7D3`, ROM `0x2A938`,
  2,716 bytes. `jr ra` is at `0x8002A7CC`, its delay slot restores the
  168-byte stack frame, and `func_8002A7D4` begins next.
- Direct callers are `func_8001F5AC` and `func_80024070`. Its sprite, text,
  progress-bar, formatting, input-sound helpers and all referenced UI globals
  are recorded in the matching unit.
- Japan has a unique structural equivalent at `0x8002A5C0`; Europe does not
  have a unique automatic mapping.

## Behavior

Render a two-column results and progress display when its state gates permit.
The routine places panel sprites, formats racer and course values, draws
progress bars and record text, highlights a newly exceeded record, and handles
the input that toggles the selected result or plays the blocked-action sound.

## Status

Behavior recovered from the USA binary and independently expressed in C; not
byte-matching. IDO 5.3 `-O2` emits 2,720 bytes for the 2,716-byte target and
first differs at byte `0x3`.
