## Behavior

For selectors `0x1A` through `0x1E`, obtain a position from the corresponding
global resource. The first four selectors raise its Z coordinate by 60 when it
starts at -157, otherwise by 30. Selector `0x1E` instead derives Z from a
per-object indexed record. Submit the resulting position directly for modes 1
and 3; for other modes, preserve the current vector separation while replacing
one endpoint with the new position. Other selectors do nothing.
