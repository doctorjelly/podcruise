## Recovered behavior
The routine samples a free-running 64-bit counter, scales it by dividing
through a 64-bit helper by 64, then scales the quotient again through a second
64-bit helper by 3000, and stores that eight-byte result in a single global
timing baseline.

It then registers a handler record: one call binds the record to a second
descriptor with a length of 20, and two further calls attach the same record to
event slots 4 and 9 with tags 1 and 2 respectively.

## Unit note
The eight-byte global is *defined* by this translation unit, not imported.
That is decisive for the byte match: when the symbol is external the compiler
materialises its address twice and the body is 152 bytes; with the definition
in the unit the address is materialised once and the body is the original 148
bytes. Verified byte-identical in all four versions by placing the definition
at its canonical address.
