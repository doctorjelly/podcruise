# func_80003348

## Boundary and evidence

USA is VRAM `0x80003348`, ROM `0x003F48`, 628 bytes through `0x800035BC`.
The return at `0x800035B4` restores the 112-byte frame in its delay slot. Japan,
Europe, and USA Rev 1 keep the same ROM and VRAM interval. `func_80004000`
stores the routine as a callback; no direct call is catalogued.

## Behavior

Expand three signed-short vertices into float vectors. For selector two, also
expand a fourth vertex; when the four-point test succeeds, construct and submit
the two planes formed by the shared edge and that fourth point. Otherwise,
when the three-point test succeeds, construct and submit one plane, reversing
the last two vertices only for selector one. Degenerate inputs produce no
submission.

## Status

Byte-matching C. Local IDO 5.3 `-O2` comparisons reproduced all 628 bytes
exactly for USA, Japan, Europe, and USA Rev 1; the regional linker definitions
account for their relocated callee and global.
