# func_8003EC40

## Boundary
USA VRAM 0x8003EC40-0x8003ECAF (0x70 = 112 bytes), ROM 0x3F840.
0x30-byte frame: nine outgoing argument slots plus the saved return link. Ends
with a return and an empty delay slot. The preceding function `func_8003EA84`
returns at 0x8003EC38 and none of its branches reach the label. 132 direct call
sites, so this is a widely used convenience wrapper.

## Callers / callees
132 callers. Calls `func_8003EA84` once and returns immediately afterwards.

## Argument widths (established from the loads, not assumed)
Arguments one and two are sign-extended from sixteen bits, so they are signed
shorts. Arguments three and four are masked to eight bits, so they are unsigned
bytes. The fifth and sixth incoming stack arguments are read with a byte load
from the low byte of their word slots, so they too are unsigned bytes. The
seventh is read as a full word. All four register arguments also produce dead
stores into the caller-provided home slots, which is IDO's usual signature for
sub-word parameters.

The callee's own body confirms these widths independently: it re-reads its first
two stack-homed arguments as halfwords and the next four as bytes.

## Recovered behavior
A pass-through wrapper. It forwards its seven arguments unchanged to the general
entry point and supplies the two trailing arguments that the general form needs:
minus one for the eighth and zero for the ninth. In the callee those two select
the default variant of the operation (the ninth chooses between two separate
record pools and the eighth is a signed selector tested for being negative), so
this wrapper is the "default pool, default selector" spelling that most call
sites want.
