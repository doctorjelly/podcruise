## Behavior
The routine is a convenience wrapper. It reserves a 64-byte scratch buffer on
its own stack, hands that buffer plus its four single-precision arguments to
the builder routine, which fills the buffer with a transform, and then passes
its two pointer arguments together with the freshly built buffer to the
consumer routine. Nothing is returned and no global state is touched. All four
scalar arguments are single-precision floats; arguments three and four arrive
in integer registers and are moved across to the coprocessor unchanged, which
is the ordinary calling-convention effect of float arguments following a
pointer argument.
