## Boundary and evidence

delay slot. Its direct caller is `func_80045144`. The unique equivalents are

## Behavior

For each of six enabled entries, obtain origins from three transform lists and
cast the same downward ray from each. Around every query, enable and then
disable the collision mode. A hit replaces the transform's forward axis with
the surface normal, rebuilds the remaining axes, moves the transform to the hit
point, and enables its node. A miss disables that node. Store the third query's
distance in the entry's result slot.
