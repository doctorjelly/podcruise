# func_8008EE04

## Verified facts

- The USA body spans VRAM `0x8008EE04` through `0x8008EEE3`, ROM `0x8FA04`,
  224 bytes. `jr ra` is at `0x8008EEDC` and its delay slot is `nop`.
- `func_800887D0` calls it directly, and `func_8008D72C` registers it as a
  handler callback. It has no direct-address callee or global; callbacks are
  loaded from offset 8 of the attached object.
- Japan and Europe equivalents begin at `0x8008FAF4` and `0x80094354`.

## Behavior

Dispatch state commands to an attached object. Commands 1 and 3 replace or
publish stored references; command 4 marks the state active and resets its
completion field; command 9 marks completion. Commands with an attached object
are forwarded through its callback with the original command value. The
attached object is read only in commands 4 and 9 and in the default forwarding
path.

## Status

Byte-matching C. IDO 5.3 `-O2` emits the exact 224-byte USA body, including the
command-block order, command-3 branch delay slots, indirect-callback delay
slots, return, and final `nop` delay slot.
