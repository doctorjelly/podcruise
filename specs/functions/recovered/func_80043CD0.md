# func_80043CD0

## Verified facts

- The USA body spans VRAM `0x80043CD0` through `0x800441DB`, ROM `0x448D0`,
  1292 bytes. `jr ra` is at `0x800441D4` with a nop delay slot. The nop at
  `0x800441DC` is alignment padding; `func_800441E0` begins next.
- No direct `jal` targets the routine. Its address occurs once in the USA data
  table at ROM `0xA2D44`, establishing indirect dispatch. Its state helpers and
  globals are recorded in the matching unit.
- Japan and Europe equivalents begin at `0x800444D8` and `0x80048670`.

## Behavior

This message handler initializes and resets a large actor-state record, assigns
an associated actor, and handles sweep, death, shake, and control-transition
messages. It copies event vectors and actor-derived parameters into persistent
state, invokes transition helpers where required, and returns whether it
recognized the message tag.

## Status

Behavior recovered from the USA binary and independently expressed in C; not
byte-matching. IDO 5.3 `-O2` emits the exact 1292-byte function symbol and first
differs at byte `0x3`; 248 instruction words differ across 20 blocks. The
post-return alignment nop is deliberately excluded from the C body.
