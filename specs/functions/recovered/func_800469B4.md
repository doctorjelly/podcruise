## Compiler blocker

The linked IDO 5.3 `-O2` symbol is exactly 1,040 bytes but differs from
the USA ROM in five words, at offsets `0xA8`, `0xB8`, `0x100`, `0x104`, and
`0x10C`. All five are the same switch-index register choice: ROM loads
`race->mode` into `$v0`, while C uses `$t9`, including the range test and
jump-table shift. A prior local-value variant caused broad divergence.
Two further bounded variants—redundant comma-expression and unsigned cast
at the first switch—compiled to the unchanged five-word mismatch and were
restored. Do not claim an exact C match or retry these source spellings.

A 60-second stack-aware permutation pilot used a 1,040-byte ROM target
whose first and last words were checked against the canonical slice. Its
lowest raw scorer candidate collapsed into a 40-byte infinite-loop body;
the independent linked verifier rejected it at 40/1,040 bytes. No
permutation candidate was retained. Raw scorer improvement alone is not
evidence of a match for this unit.
