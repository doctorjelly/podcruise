# func_80017824

## Boundary
VRAM 0x80017824-0x80017874, ROM 0x18424, size 80 bytes. Ends with a return whose
delay slot is empty. 19 direct callers target the entry; boundary confirmed.

## Callers / callees
19 callers. Calls func_800175E0 (a workspace pointer plus four single-precision
values, the last passed on the stack) and func_80015724 (three arguments).

## Globals
None.

## Regional addresses
jp 0x80017954 / ROM 0x018554 (callees func_80017710, func_80015854);
eu 0x80017CB4 / ROM 0x0188B4 (callees func_80017A70, func_80015BB4);
lrg_rev1 shares the USA addresses.

## Behavior
A convenience wrapper around a two-step operation. It reserves a 64-byte
workspace on its own stack, hands that workspace plus its four single-precision
arguments to a builder routine that fills the workspace, and then passes its
first (pointer) argument, the filled workspace and its final word-sized argument
to a consumer routine. Nothing is returned.

Argument 4 is single-precision, not integer: it is forwarded untouched in the
argument register it arrives in, and typing it as an integer costs the entry
spill the original performs.

## Status
exact (USA, Japan, Europe, lrg_rev1).
