# func_80045694

## Verified facts

- The USA body spans VRAM `0x80045694` through `0x80045C87`, ROM `0x46294`,
  1524 bytes. `jr ra` is at `0x80045C80`; its delay slot restores the
  288-byte stack frame. `func_80045C88` begins next.
- Its direct caller is `func_8004611C`. Its model lookup, display-list patch,
  transform, object, and effect helpers and globals are in the matching unit.
- Japan and Europe equivalents begin at `0x80045EA4` and `0x8004A034`.

## Behavior

The routine configures up to six stage slots from their selected model kinds.
It loads and patches each model pair, applies initial transforms and visibility,
connects three model nodes and two effect nodes to stage records, and disables
invalid selections. An enabled slot may also reveal an auxiliary model object.

## Status

Behavior recovered from the USA binary and independently expressed in C; not
byte-matching. IDO 5.3 `-O2` emits 1520 bytes for the 1524-byte target and first
differs at byte `0x9A`; 143 instruction words differ across 16 blocks. Regional
binaries were used only for structural and relocation confirmation.
