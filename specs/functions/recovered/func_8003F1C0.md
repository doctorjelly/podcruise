# func_8003F1C0

## Verified boundary and references

The USA entry is VRAM `0x8003F1C0`, ROM `0x3FDC0`, and the complete body is
316 bytes. Its first six instructions precede the stack adjustment but belong
to the same call target. The return is at `0x8003F2F4` with a `nop` delay slot
at `0x8003F2F8`; the word at `0x8003F2FC` is alignment padding. Both direct
calls are in `func_8003EFDC`.

The function calls `func_8008A6B4` to format text and `func_8003EC40` to draw
it. It reads the float at `D_800AAB9C` and selects the format strings beginning
at `D_800AAB6C` and `D_800AAB7C`. The corresponding callee entries observed
from the regional callers are `0x8003FB44` in Japan and `0x80043B60` in Europe;
these are relocation evidence only.

## Behavior

Convert a floating time value into whole minutes, whole seconds, and a
three-digit fractional part, including carry when rounding produces 1000.
Choose the format that includes minutes only when the minute count is nonzero,
then draw the resulting text at the requested coordinates and four byte-sized
color components.

## Compiler status

**Behavior-recovered, not byte-matching.** After correcting the three absolute
symbol addresses to the data actually read by the USA body, IDO 5.3 emits the
same 316-byte length but still differs in eight instruction words beginning at
offset `0x40`; the remainder is temporary integer-register allocation. No C
substitution or byte-match claim is made.
