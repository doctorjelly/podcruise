## Recovered behavior

The function copies one whole 44-byte record out of the record array that
begins 0x14 bytes into the `D_80113680` region and into the record array at
`D_80113E60`.

Argument one selects the destination record in the `D_80113E60` array.
Argument two selects the source record in the `D_80113680` array. Neither
index is validated; the caller is responsible for supplying a valid slot.

The copy is a plain structure assignment - all 44 bytes are transferred with
no interpretation of their contents and no other side effects.

Nothing is returned.
