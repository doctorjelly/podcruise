# func_80088488

## Boundary
VRAM 0x80088488-0x800884E8 (96 bytes), ROM 0x89088. The return's delay slot
carries the stack release. One direct caller.

## Callers / callees
Callers: 1. Callees: func_80088020, func_80088050.

## Globals

## Regional addresses
jp 0x80089178 (ROM 0x89D78); eu 0x8008D9D8 (ROM 0x8E5D8); lrg_rev1 as USA.

## Behavior
Drains a container. The container holds a head entry pointer at byte offset
0x14 and a companion sub-object beginning at byte offset 0x04. While the head
entry is non-null, the routine first tears the entry down and then detaches it
from the container's sub-object, re-reading the head each pass so that the loop
ends only once the container reports no remaining entry. With an empty container
it returns immediately.

## Status
exact in USA, and byte-identical for Japan, Europe and lrg_rev1.
