# func_80046DC4

## Verified facts

- The USA body spans VRAM `0x80046DC4` through `0x8004791F`, ROM `0x479C4`,
  2,908 bytes. `jr ra` is at `0x80047918`, its delay slot restores the
  192-byte stack frame, and `func_80047920` begins next.
- Direct callers are `func_80047DB0`, `func_8001F5AC`, and `func_80024070`.
  Its sprite, text, random-number helpers and scoreboard globals are recorded
  in the matching unit.
- Neither Japan nor Europe has a unique automatic structural mapping.

## Behavior

Draw a seven-row scoreboard or performance-gauge panel. It fades the panel for
one state, places the labels and active-row marker, and compares paired values
for every row. Equal portions use the normal color; positive and negative
remainders become differently colored, randomly pulsing bar segments.

## Status

byte-matching. IDO 5.3 `-O2` emits the target length of 2,908 bytes but first
differs at byte `0x49`; equal length is not an exact match.
