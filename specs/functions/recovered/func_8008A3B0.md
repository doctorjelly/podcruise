# func_8008A3B0

## Boundary
VRAM 0x8008A3B0-0x8008A41F (112 bytes). Ends with `jr $ra` and an empty delay
slot. 2 callers; boundary confirmed.

## Callers / callees

## Globals
D_800A7F54: a pointer to a record whose first halfword holds a set of flags.

## Behavior
The routine takes one byte-wide flag. It acquires a context, then sets or clears
bit 5 of the flag halfword at the front of the shared record according to
whether the argument is non-zero, and finally releases the retained context.
Nothing is returned. The argument is read back from its home slot as a single
byte, which is what fixes its width.

`register`.
