- Boundary confirmed correct: 13 direct call sites target `0x8000AC60`, the

## Recovered behaviour

The exact counterpart of `func_8000AC34`. Given a signed 16-bit slot index and
a 32-bit bit mask, it turns off every bit of the mask in the 32-bit flag word
at offset 0x14 of that slot in the object table: the mask is complemented,
AND-ed with the current flag word, and the result written back. Bits outside
the mask are preserved. There is no bounds check. Nothing is returned.

The extra instruction relative to `func_8000AC34` is the complement of the
mask, which is why this function is 48 rather than 44 bytes.

## Status

Exact. Byte-identical against USA, Japan, Europe and lrg_rev1 at 48 bytes,
first attempt.
