# func_8003609C

## Boundary and evidence

USA is VRAM `0x8003609C`, ROM `0x036C9C`, 632 bytes through
`func_80036314` at `0x80036314`. The `jr $ra` at `0x800362DC` writes the last
command word in its delay slot; the following 48 bytes are compiler-generated
dispatch tables, not a separate callable routine. The unique equivalents are
Japan `0x80036AFC` / ROM `0x0376FC` and Europe `0x8003AA0C` / ROM `0x03B60C`.
`func_80036314` is the direct caller.

## Behavior

Compare four two-bit fields in the requested low byte against a cached state.
For every changed field, update only that field in the cache and append the
corresponding other-mode command to the shared graphics cursor. Unsupported
encodings update the cache without emitting a command. If the complete words
already agree, return without touching either object.

## Status

Byte-matching C. Local IDO 5.3 `-O2` comparisons reproduce the 632-byte
function, including its post-return tables, for USA, Japan, Europe, and USA
Rev 1.
