# func_80089800

## Boundary
USA VRAM 0x80089800-0x80089864, 100 bytes, ROM 0x8A400. Ends with the return
jump plus an empty delay slot. One direct caller, which passes the literal 1.

## Compilation
This body is in an -O1 region: every local lives in a stack slot, the argument
is re-read from its home slot in each arm of the branch, and the result word is
stored once and reloaded at the return. The unit must be built with -O1.

## Callers
One call site, passing 1.

## Behavior
The routine selects a display or playback mode. It first performs a
preparation step, then records the requested mode in a byte-wide global,
clamping anything from five upward down to four. It marks a second byte-wide
global with the sentinel 0xFE and performs a commit step. It always reports
success, returning zero.

The parameter is declared in the old style: the caller passes a full word and
the body reads only its low byte, which is why the argument is homed as a word
but consumed as an unsigned byte.

## Status
Exact in USA, Japan, Europe and lrg_rev1 (all with -O1).
