## Verified boundary and references

The USA body begins at VRAM `0x8007134C`, ROM `0x71F4C`, and spans 1,236
bytes. Its `jr ra` is at `0x80071818`, the `nop` at `0x8007181C` is the delay
slot, and `func_80071820` begins next. The sole direct USA caller is

`func_80031B70`, `func_8003B184`, `func_8003FA24`, `func_80065810`,
`func_800704A8`, `func_80070BBC`, `func_80070E98`, `func_80075648`, and

block `D_800AD884` through `D_800AD8B8`.

Normalized regional equivalents begin at `0x80071F7C` in Japan and
`0x8007682C` in Europe. LRG retains the USA address and bytes.

## Behavior

Initialize a one-shot object effect unless an earlier state routes to the
alternate initializer. Build two transforms and a tagged request, spawn the
main effect, and choose its origin from object flags or a random choice. Select
and play two randomized sounds, then clear and clamp six per-object slots. If
the final state bit is set, configure the associated handle and notification.

The source evaluates each sound's random integer before forming the outer
sound-helper call. This is behaviorally equivalent to the nested form while
making the helper/argument sequencing explicit.
