# func_800573E8

## Verified facts

- The USA body spans VRAM `0x800573E8` through `0x80057ED3`, ROM `0x57FE8`,
  2,796 bytes. `jr ra` is at `0x80057ECC`, its delay slot is `nop`, and
  `func_80057ED4` begins next.
- Its direct caller is `func_8005B3F0`. Its HUD-layout, text, sound, flag,
  random-number, formatting, and player-display helpers and all referenced
  globals are recorded in the matching unit.
- Japan has a unique structural equivalent at `0x80057FAC`; Europe does not
  have a unique automatic mapping.

## Behavior

Update and render a race-progress HUD for one racer. It selects the background
layout, prints speed and lap/progress values, fades and flashes split-time
messages, starts or stops their notification sound, colors the current count,
draws remaining status fields, delegates the per-player display, and optionally
shows a global warning.

## Status

Behavior recovered from the USA binary and independently expressed in C; not
byte-matching. IDO 5.3 `-O2` emits 2,800 bytes for the 2,796-byte target and
first differs at byte `0x51`.
