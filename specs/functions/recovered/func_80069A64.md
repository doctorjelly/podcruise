## Compact behavior (inference from instructions and source)

The routine computes alignment between the object's direction and an input
direction. Outside the permitted range it clears the side vector, damps the
stored vector, copies the result, and clears the object's angle. Otherwise it
forms cross products, normalizes vectors, derives an angle and speed-dependent
amount, clamps and scales the resulting vector, and writes the updated object
vector. It then derives a planar signed angle from normalized side/object
vectors and stores that angle in the object record.

The field offsets used by the source (`0x194`, `0x1C4`, `0x1F8`, and the
documented surrounding scalar fields) are behavioral layout labels, not claims
about original authored type names.
