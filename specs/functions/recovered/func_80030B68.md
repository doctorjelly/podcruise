# func_80030B68

## Boundary

- USA VRAM range `0x80030B68` .. `0x80030B8F` (inclusive), 40 bytes, 10 instructions.
- ROM offset `0x31768`.
- The body ends with the return instruction at `0x80030B88`; its delay slot at
  `0x80030B8C` holds the last of the three stores, so the final store is part of
  this function.
- The assignment sheet listed the size as 80. That figure is twice the true
  size; the shared inventory doubles both `insns` and `size` for every entry in
  this file. The entry address and ROM offset in the assignment are correct.
- Not a shared tail: no branch from the preceding function reaches into this
  range, and there are no internal branch targets at all.

## Callers

One direct caller, at `0x8005EFD0`.

## Callees

None. The function is a leaf.

## Globals and access widths

Three independent 32-bit words, each read once with a full-word load:

- `D_800D9DC8`
- `D_800D9DCC`
- `D_800D9DD0`

They are read through separate high/low address pairs, which is what the
compiler emits when a global is touched exactly once, so nothing here implies
the three words belong to one structure.

## Recovered behavior

The function publishes three saved global words through three caller-supplied
word pointers. It takes three pointer arguments and writes, in argument order,
the first global to the first pointer, the third global to the second pointer,
and the second global to the third pointer. Nothing is read back, nothing is
returned, and no argument is checked for validity: the caller is expected to
pass three writable word locations.

The mapping is deliberate and not a transcription slip - the second and third
globals are delivered to the second and third out-parameters in swapped order,
and the byte-exact rebuild only reproduces the original with that swap in place.

## Status

Exact in USA, Japan and Europe.
