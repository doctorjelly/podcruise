# func_80060DE4

## Verified facts

- The canonical USA body begins at VRAM `0x80060DE4`, ROM `0x619E4`, and is
  `0x148C` (5,260) bytes. The `jr ra` is at `0x80062268`; its delay slot at
  `0x8006226C` is a `nop`. The four-byte floating-point prefix
  `func_80062270` follows immediately.
- Recovered callers include `func_80026644`, `func_80028E78`,
  `func_80062270`, `func_80063084`, `func_8006373C`, `func_80063AA0`, and
  `func_80063E4C`. Together they pass transition codes from zero through
  `0x43` while creating, turning, advancing, or externally commanding the
  same object type. Two cases also call this routine recursively for a second
  tagged object.
- The object layout agrees with those callers: mode fields at `0x08/0x0C`,
  current transition and timer at `0x14/0x18`, a limit at `0x1C`, a
  null-terminated node list at `0x34`, positions at `0x44/0x50`, angles at
  `0x68/0x6C`, speed at `0x78`, animation selector at `0x88`, and state fields
  at `0xA0/0xA4`.
- The body contains three verified computed switches. The primary table has 67
  entries for codes `0x00..0x42`; two five-entry tables select animations for
  codes `0x01` and `0x12`. Their words were read from the local canonical ROM
  at `0xADCB8`, `0xADDC4`, and `0xADDF0`, then checked against every target
  block and delay slot.
- The function makes 56 static calls. The IDO 5.3 candidate emits the same 56
  targets in the same order, including random draws, sound selection, tagged
  lookup, recursive transitions, range calculation, and node updates.
- JP `func_80061954` at ROM `0x62554` and EU `func_800662C4` at ROM `0x66EC4`
  are both exactly `0x148C` bytes. Their prologues, three switch shapes,
  callers, helper sequence, terminal `jr`, and `nop` delay slot match the USA
  structure after relocation. They were used only as confirmation.

## Behavioral specification

Change an animated object's transition state and configure the node-list
parameters needed to realize that state. Invalid objects and the disabled mode
return after only the permitted state/timer writes. Other modes first normalize
the stored speed, refresh proximity state, and mark every attached node as
being reconfigured.

The transition code selects an animation variant and a small set of range,
speed, and update flags. Several transitions randomize among equivalent
variants, choose context-dependent voice lines, calculate a facing direction,
or coordinate a paired tagged object. The high numbered transitions form
regular animation families with different selectors and zeroed range terms.

After dispatch, mode three applies its fixed range override. Nonzero states
query the object's configured lower and upper bounds, directional states shift
those bounds according to speed, and the final seven-argument node update is
suppressed only by the two verified facing cases. If dispatch changed the
effective speed, the new value is stored and propagated through the node list.

## Recovery notes

- Live argument registers at calls to `func_800636D0`, `func_80009524`, and
  `func_8001535C` are not additional parameters; their recovered callees prove
  one-, two-, and three-argument contracts respectively.
- Code zero temporarily clears the stored speed while removing the node mark.
  The common epilogue then restores the normalized speed and propagates it if
  it differs, exactly as the assembly does.
- Code `0x1B` scans to the node-list terminator without using the resulting
  pointer. The source retains the scan rather than inventing a side effect.
- The local `m2c` run was only a control-flow aid after supplying the three
  ignored jump tables. The committed switch, types, helper arguments, delay
  slots, and behavioral wording were independently reconstructed from the USA
  body and recovered neighboring functions.

## Status

**Behavior-recovered C, not byte-matching.** IDO 5.3 and IDO 7.1 each emit
4,840 bytes versus the canonical 5,260 and first differ at byte `0x3`. The
exact 56-call sequence and regional agreement support behavioral recovery, but
the USA split must continue assembling the original body.
