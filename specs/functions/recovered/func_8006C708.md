# func_8006C708 and func_8006C828

## Verified facts

- `func_8006C708` spans USA VRAM `0x8006C708` through the return delay slot at
  `0x8006C824`, ROM `0x6D308`, for 288 bytes. The branch target named
  `func_8006C81C` is its final return path, not a separate function.
- `func_8006C828` immediately follows and spans through its return delay slot
  at `0x8006C94C`, ROM `0x6D428`, for 296 bytes. The branch target named
  `func_8006C944` is likewise internal. `func_8006C950` begins next with a new
  stack frame.
- The first routine is called by `func_8006B568` and the merged body beginning
  at `func_8006E6F8`. The second is called once by that merged body. Neither
  routine calls another function.
- Both routines read six-element arrays in their object: floating-point levels
  at offset `0x288` and flag words at `0x2A0`. The second also reads the
  constants at `D_800AD600`, `D_800AD608`, and `D_800AD60C`.
- Japan places the entries at `0x8006D278` and `0x8006D398`; Europe places them
  at `0x80071BE8` and `0x80071D08`. Both preserve the USA spacing and internal
  branch-label arrangement.

## Behavior

`func_8006C708` summarizes active flags by object half. It sets result bit one
when any of slots zero through two has flag `0x4` or `0x10`, and result bit two
when any of slots three through five does. The two bits may be returned
together.

`func_8006C828` compares each slot level against the same double-precision
threshold. Every qualifying slot in the first half subtracts a fixed float
step from the result; every qualifying slot in the second half adds the step.
It therefore returns a signed balance between the two three-slot groups.

## Status

Exact in canonical USA. A linked IDO 5.3 `-O2` comparison reproduces all 584
bytes of the two functions. The compact six-iteration loops become the
observed two-element remainder followed by a four-way unrolled body; their
source literals also recreate the constant pool beginning at `0x800AD600`.
Only USA is integrated here.
