# func_80041F40

## Verified facts

- The USA body spans VRAM `0x80041F40` through `0x8004296F`, ROM `0x42B40`,
  2,608 bytes. `jr ra` is at `0x80042968`, its delay slot is `nop`, and
  `func_80042970` begins next.
- The direct caller is `func_80043598`. Its vector, matrix, normalization,
  orientation, and game-mode helpers and all referenced globals are recorded
  in the matching unit.
- Japan and Europe equivalents begin at `0x80042748` and `0x800468E0`.

## Behavior

Update a chase-camera actor from its attached pod and course settings. It
chooses offsets from pod flags and camera mode, scales them with speed and
orientation, constructs near and far camera transforms, smooths lateral
placement, derives roll from the pod orientation, and handles the special
inverted-pod transform before publishing both camera matrices.

byte-matching. IDO 5.3 `-O2` emits 2,624 bytes for the 2,608-byte target and
first differs at byte `0xD`.
