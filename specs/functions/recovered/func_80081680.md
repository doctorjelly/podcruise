## Behavior

The function records whether its first floating-point argument is positive
and uses its magnitude for the calculation. Starting with that magnitude, it
repeatedly multiplies the accumulator by the original magnitude until the
requested integer exponent is reached; exponents below two leave it unchanged.
It then applies the scale argument and caps the result at the supplied upper
limit. Finally it restores the original sign, treating zero and unordered
comparisons as nonpositive.
