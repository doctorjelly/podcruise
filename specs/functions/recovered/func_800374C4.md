# func_800374C4

## Verified facts

- The USA body spans VRAM `0x800374C4` through `0x80037BEF`, ROM `0x380C4`,
  1836 bytes. `jr ra` is at `0x80037BE8`; its delay slot restores the
  208-byte stack frame. `func_80037BF0` begins next.
- Its only direct caller is `func_80038388`. Its display-list, material,
  texture, matrix, geometry, and lighting helpers and globals are recorded in
  the matching unit.
- Japan and Europe equivalents begin at `0x80037F24` and `0x8003BE34`.

## Behavior

The routine prepares a render pass, selects the active item count and render
path, and emits the required display-list state. For each item it derives mode
bits, resolves matrix, material, texture, and light state, invokes the selected
display list, and finishes with the pass's geometry commands.

## Status

Behavior recovered from the USA binary and independently expressed in C; not
byte-matching. IDO 5.3 `-O2` emits the exact 1836-byte length and first differs
at byte `0x3`; 351 instruction words differ across 47 blocks. Regional binaries
were used only for structural and relocation confirmation.
