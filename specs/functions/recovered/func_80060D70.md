# func_80060D70

## Boundary
VRAM 0x80060D70-0x80060DE3, 116 bytes, frame 0x28, empty return delay slot.
Twelve direct callers, all targeting the entry.

## Callers / callees
Calls func_80009524 twice and func_800093B0 once.

## Globals
None.

## Regional addresses
- jp 0x800618E0 (rom 0x624E0); both callees keep their USA addresses.
- eu 0x80066250 (rom 0x66E50); both callees keep their USA addresses.
- lrg_rev1 identical to USA.

## Behavior
Takes five word-sized parameters, the fifth arriving on the stack, and returns
nothing. It performs two guard queries against selector 0: the first asks about
flag 0x10 and the routine does nothing unless it reports true; the second asks
about flag 0x20 and the routine does nothing if it reports true. Only when both
guards pass does it forward all five parameters to the worker routine, prefixed
by the constants 2 and 0 (so the worker sees seven arguments, three of them
passed on the stack). All five parameters are homed on entry because they must
survive the guard calls.

## Status
exact (USA, JP, EU and lrg_rev1 all byte-identical).
