# func_8007B06C

## Verified boundary and references

The canonical USA body occupies VRAM `0x8007B06C` through `0x8007B25B`, ROM
`0x7BC6C`, for 496 bytes. It returns with `jr ra` at `0x8007B254`; the delay
slot at `0x8007B258` is `nop`. No direct `jal` targets the entry. Its address
occurs as a word in the ROM data at `0xA2C28`, consistent with indirect use.

The body calls `func_8000E9BC`, `func_8003E0A0`, `func_80017918`, and
`func_80017BA8`. It reads the double-precision frame value `D_80120BF0` and
otherwise operates through its object argument. The unique normalized
equivalents begin at `0x8007BD0C` in Japan and `0x8008054C` in Europe; they
support the boundary and shared structure but are not used to claim a regional
compiler match.

## Behavior

Return immediately when the destination or divisor field is absent. Otherwise
form a ratio from two object scalars. When an attached state exists, copy and
clamp four byte channels, fade the fourth channel during the first quarter of
the ratio, submit the channels, and advance the attached state by a value
derived from the global frame duration. Finally derive a shrinking scale from
the ratio, build a temporary transform from the object's matrix, and copy that
transform to the destination.

## Compiler status

**Byte-matching C for USA retail and USA LRG revision.** IDO 5.3 with this
unit's recorded `-O2 -mips2` flags reproduces all 496 bytes independently for
both ROMs. The local declaration order fixes the original stack layout, and
the three-step scale update fixes the final instruction schedule. No byte-match
claim is made here for Japan or Europe.
