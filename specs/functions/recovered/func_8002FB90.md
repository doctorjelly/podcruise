# func_8002FB90

## Verified facts

- The USA body occupies VRAM `0x8002FB90` through `0x8002FC57`, ROM
  `0x30790`, for 200 bytes. The `jr ra` at `0x8002FC50` restores the 128-byte
  frame in its delay slot.
- Recovered direct callers are `func_80030FF8`, `func_800464E8`,
  `func_8004BE90`, and `func_80060880`. The only direct callee is
  `func_8008A6B4`.
- The body reads `D_800A285C`, `D_800D9DBC`, and the sentinel-terminated
  `D_800D9DD8` table, and passes `D_800A9FD0` as the formatter argument.
- The Japan and Europe equivalents are `func_800305F0` and `func_80033150`.

## Behavior

Walk the word table from index one until its zero sentinel. When the mode
global is enabled, format each visited index into a stack buffer. If the last
nonzero table word is below the comparison global, return; otherwise remain in
the terminal loop.

## Compiler status

Behavior recovered and independently expressed in C; not byte-matching. IDO
5.3 `-O2` emits 208 bytes for the 200-byte USA body. The current C keeps the
address of `D_800A285C` in `$s4`, while the ROM rematerializes the address after
the formatter call and therefore needs only `$s0`-`$s3`. Direct-global,
volatile-global, and explicit `while` forms did not reproduce that allocation
and were reverted.
