# func_8006E42C

## Boundary and references

The USA body occupies VRAM `0x8006E42C` through `0x8006E6F7`, ROM
`0x6F02C`, for 716 bytes. The return is at `0x8006E6F0`; restoring the
80-byte frame in its delay slot completes the function. Its only direct USA
caller is at `0x8006E9C0` inside `func_8006E6F8`.

The function calls `func_80008BC4` and `func_80082BE0`. It reads controller
button and analog tables `D_800D76F0`, `D_800D7700`, and `D_800D7730`, mode
flags at `D_8009B7D8`, five floating constants, and fields of the supplied
object. Japan maps structurally to VRAM `0x8006EF9C` / ROM `0x6FB9C`, and
Europe to VRAM `0x8007390C` / ROM `0x7450C`; those mappings are relocation
evidence, not byte-match claims.

## Behavior

Read the selected controller's ready and pressed state. In the alternate
two-controller mode, choose one of two controller pairs from an object flag
and combine each pair's analog/button state. Drive a three-state hold gesture:
enter timing once the analog value crosses its threshold, accumulate elapsed
time while held, and report success when the later button press arrives. A
successful gesture also starts a sound with a randomized pitch. Invalid mode
flags or a released/weak input reset the gesture state.

## Status

**Byte-matching C: USA and LRG.** IDO 5.3 `-O2` reproduces all 716 bytes in
both builds. Two source-layout details are required by the original scheduler:
the controller selection and following constant load share a source line, and
the independent ready/pressed short-circuit assignments do not. No exact claim
is made for Japan or Europe.
