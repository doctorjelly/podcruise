# func_80067C70

## Verified facts

- The USA body spans VRAM `0x80067C70` through `0x8006840F`, ROM `0x68870`,
  1952 bytes. The 512-byte stack frame is restored at `0x80068404`; `jr ra` is
  at `0x80068408` with a nop delay slot. `func_80068410` begins next.
- Its only direct caller is `func_8006C0D4`. Its rotation, trigonometric,
  vector-normalization helper and threshold global are recorded in the
  matching unit; `sqrtf` is emitted as the compiler intrinsic.
- Japan and Europe equivalents begin at `0x800687E0` and `0x8006D150`.

## Behavior

The routine rotates an object's orientation basis by the requested angles. In
the active motion states it builds and combines axis, pitch, and yaw rotations;
otherwise it applies yaw alone. It periodically renormalizes all three basis
vectors to limit drift, decrements the normalization timer, and copies the
input velocity into the object.

## Status

Behavior recovered from the USA binary and independently expressed in C; not
byte-matching. IDO 5.3 `-O2` emits 1936 bytes for the 1952-byte target and first
differs at byte `0x2D`; 456 instruction words differ across 14 blocks. Regional
binaries were used only for structural and relocation confirmation.
