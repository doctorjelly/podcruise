# func_80088020

## Boundary

- VRAM 0x80088020 .. 0x80088050 (exclusive), ROM 0x88C20, size 48 bytes.
- Leaf, no frame. Ends with the return instruction and an empty delay slot.
- Three padding words separate it from func_80087FC0 above; func_80088050
  starts immediately after. Twelve direct call sites all target 0x80088020,
  and no branch from the preceding routine reaches inside. Boundary confirmed.

## Callers and callees

- Twelve direct call sites. Leaf.
- One argument: a pointer to a two-word record.

## Globals and access widths

- None. Only the record reached through the argument and the two records it
  points at are touched, all with 32-bit loads and stores.
- Record layout used here: a 32-bit pointer at 0x00 and a 32-bit pointer at
  0x04, the two directions of a doubly linked chain.

## Recovered behavior

Detaches one record from the doubly linked chain it belongs to.

1. If the record has a predecessor, the predecessor's forward pointer is made
   to point at the record's successor.
2. If the record has a successor, the successor's backward pointer is made to
   point at the record's predecessor.
3. Either side may be absent, so both tests are independent; a record with no
   neighbours is left untouched. The record's own two pointers are not
   cleared, so the caller still sees its old neighbours.

Both fields are re-read from the record after the first store rather than kept
in registers, which is what produces the repeated loads in the original.
