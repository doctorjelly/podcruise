# `func_80083190` USA compiler audit

The configured IDO 5.3 `-O2 -mips2` source compiles to the canonical 548-byte
size and address, but differs in 60 words (first byte `0x75`). The ROM first
loads the list head into `t6` and moves it into `a0` in the loop-branch delay
slot; C carries it in `a2` until the call delay slot. The matrix and saved
stack slots also differ by eight bytes.

Three bounded source-shape tests found no exact match: swapping the unused pad
and matrix declarations, explicitly carrying the current list head through
the loop, and shortening the unused pad by two floats. The first two left the
baseline comparison unchanged; the pad change worsened it to 61 words with a
first difference at `0x03`. The source was restored unchanged. Continue with
new stack-allocation or loop-scheduling evidence, not a blind register search.
