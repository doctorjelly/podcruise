All six stores are half-word stores; nothing is read back. `D_800A3D4C` and

nearby.

## Recovered behaviour

The routine is a six-field setter with a "leave unchanged" convention: a
negative value means "skip this field". Each of the six arguments is tested
independently, and only when it is zero or positive is its low half stored into
the corresponding 16-bit configuration field. Argument one goes to the standalone
field `D_800A3D4C`, argument two to `D_800A3D50`, and arguments three through six
to elements zero through three of the four-entry array `D_800A3D44`. No field is
read, the tests are independent of one another, and there is no return value.

## Status

Exact. Byte-identical in USA, Japan, Europe and lrg_rev1, at 96 bytes.
