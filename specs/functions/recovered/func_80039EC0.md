# func_80039EC0

## Verified facts

- The USA body spans VRAM `0x80039EC0` through `0x8003A43F`, ROM `0x3AAC0`,
  1408 bytes. `jr ra` is at `0x8003A438` with a nop delay slot;
  `func_8003A440` begins next.
- Direct callers are `func_8003A440`, `func_8003A478`, `func_80045314`,
  `func_800469B4`, `func_8004F358`, and `func_80052E4C`. Its display-register,
  queue, copy, and interrupt-mask helpers and globals are in the matching unit.
- The Japan equivalent begins at `0x8003A870`. No unique Europe mapping was
  established, so Europe is not used as evidence for this recovery.

## Behavior

The routine selects a display-mode record, derives the active display-register
state and screen bounds, flushes the render queue, and installs the new mode.
It then applies each enabled display mask, performs the final queue transition,
and marks display configuration complete. Re-selecting the active mode takes a
short status-only path.

## Status

Behavior recovered from the USA binary and independently expressed in C; not
byte-matching. IDO 5.3 `-O2` emits the exact 1408-byte length and first differs
at byte `0x8`; 292 instruction words differ across 13 blocks. Japan was used
only as structural and relocation evidence.
