# func_80036A1C

## Verified facts

- The USA body spans VRAM `0x80036A1C` through `0x80036F7B`, ROM `0x3761C`,
  1376 bytes. `jr ra` is at `0x80036F74`; its delay slot restores the
  176-byte stack frame. `func_80036F7C` begins next.
- Both direct callers are in `func_800374C4`. The only external data is the
  transform matrix at `D_80112E60`.
- Japan and Europe equivalents begin at `0x8003747C` and `0x8003B38C`.

## Behavior

The routine transforms all eight corners of an axis-aligned bounding box into
clip space and tracks their relationship to the three clipping axes. It rejects
invalid bounds and returns separate results for fully outside, intersecting,
and fully contained boxes, with the second argument controlling containment
tracking.
