# func_80070764

## Verified facts

- The canonical USA body spans VRAM `0x80070764` through `0x80070BBB`, ROM
  `0x71364`, 1112 bytes. It returns with `jr ra` at `0x80070BB4`; the delay
  slot at `0x80070BB8` is a nop, and `func_80070BBC` begins next.
- Direct calls occur in `func_8006BB50`, `func_8006CA2C`, and
  `func_8007CD50`. The body calls `func_80082BE0`, `func_80075648`,
  `func_80065810`, `func_80008BC4`, and `func_800093B0`.
- It accesses `D_80000318` and the constants from `D_800AD804` through
  `D_800AD830` used by its two randomized sound selections.
- The normalized Japan and Europe equivalents begin at `0x800712D4` and
  `0x80075C44`; they are structural evidence only.

## Behavior

The routine rejects objects whose state does not permit firing, arms a short
cooldown, and handles either of two firing-side flags. A fired side supplies
its position, triggers the corresponding effect, creates two particles, and
plays two independently randomized impact sounds. It finishes by selecting an
owner channel when available and submitting the fired or idle animation/event
pattern.

## Status

Behavior recovered, not byte-matching. IDO 5.3 `-O2` emits the exact target
length of 1112 bytes, but first differs at byte `0x237`. Exact comparison finds
six differing instruction words, confined to two repeated sound-call stack
spill/load orderings. Three bounded layout variants did not improve the match,
so the USA build retains assembly.
