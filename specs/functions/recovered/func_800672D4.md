# func_800672D4

## Verified facts

- The canonical USA body spans VRAM `0x800672D4` through `0x80067717`, ROM
  `0x67ED4`, 1092 bytes. It returns with `jr ra` at `0x80067710`; the delay
  slot at `0x80067714` is a nop, and `func_80067718` begins next.
- The direct caller is `func_8006A49C`. The body calls `func_8003F99C` and
  `func_80081700` and reads `D_80120BF0`, `D_800AD4C4`, and `D_800AD4C8`.
- The normalized Japan and Europe equivalents begin at `0x80067E44` and
  `0x8006C7B4`; they are structural evidence only.

## Behavior

The routine derives a contact direction from racer orientation, computes a
reach limit from ride height and lean, advances a spring-like compression
state, and clamps the resulting force. It updates contact flags, sends a hit
message on a sufficiently hard first impact, then adds the scaled contact
direction to the caller's output vector.

## Status

Behavior recovered, not byte-matching. IDO 5.3 `-O2` emits 1096 bytes against
the 1092-byte target and first differs at byte `0x3`: the generated frame is
136 bytes while the original frame is 128 bytes. Broad stack-layout changes
remain, so the USA build retains assembly.
