# func_80027150

## Verified facts

- The USA body spans VRAM `0x80027150` through `0x80027A0B`, ROM `0x27D50`,
  2236 bytes. `jr ra` is at `0x80027A04`; its delay slot restores the
  208-byte stack frame. `func_80027A0C` begins next.
- Its only direct caller is `func_8004A880`. Its input, audio, camera, vector,
  matrix, scene-object, entity, random-number, and transition helpers and
  globals are recorded in the matching unit.
- Japan and Europe equivalents begin at `0x800279E0` and `0x8002982C`.

## Behavior

The routine runs a timed presentation sequence. It initializes audio, camera
endpoints, and three rotating entries; advances through six timer-controlled
camera changes; counts down to the next interface state; updates the shared
scene model and paired entry objects; and lets valid input end the sequence
early.

## Status

Behavior recovered from the USA binary and independently expressed in C; not
byte-matching. IDO 5.3 `-O2` emits 2200 bytes for the 2236-byte target and first
differs at byte `0x3`; 552 instruction words differ across six broad blocks.
Regional binaries were used only for structural and relocation confirmation.
