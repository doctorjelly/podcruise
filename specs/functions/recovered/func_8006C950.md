# func_8006C950

## Verified facts

- The canonical USA body spans VRAM `0x8006C950` through the return delay slot
  at `0x8006CA28`, ROM `0x6D550`, for 220 bytes. `func_8006CA2C` starts next.
- Its only direct USA caller is the merged body beginning at `func_8006E6F8`;
  the call is at `0x8006F234`.
- It calls `func_80082BE0` and `func_80071820`, once each in a six-entry loop.
  It reads flag words at object offset `0x2A0` and constants
  `D_800AD610`, `D_800AD614`, and `D_80120BF0`.
- The structurally equivalent Japan and Europe entries are `0x8006D4C0` and
  `0x80071E30`, respectively.

## Behavior

The routine visits six object channels. For every channel whose flag word has
bit `0x8` set, it obtains a signed random sample, scales and biases the sample
with the two local float constants, applies twice the shared double constant,
and passes the resulting level with the object and channel index to
`func_80071820`. Inactive channels are skipped.

## Status

Byte-matching C for canonical USA. IDO 5.3 with the recorded `ido53_o2`
profile reproduces all 220 bytes exactly, including the `jr ra` delay slot.
The live-range split in the source preserves the original assignment of the
two long-lived floating-point constants. Japan and Europe were used only to
confirm structure and relocation.
