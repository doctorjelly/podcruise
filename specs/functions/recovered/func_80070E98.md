# func_80070E98

## Verified facts

- The USA body spans VRAM `0x80070E98` through `0x8007134B`, ROM `0x71A98`,
  1204 bytes. `jr ra` is at `0x80071344`, followed by a nop delay slot;
  `func_8007134C` begins next and is its direct caller.
- The body calls `func_8003B184`, `func_80070BBC`, `func_800704A8`,
  `func_80065810`, `func_80075648`, `func_80082BE0`, and `func_80008BC4`.
  Its sound thresholds occupy `D_800AD850` through `D_800AD880`.
- JP and EU equivalents begin at `0x80071AC8` and `0x80076378`.

## Behavior

The routine latches a one-shot state flag, builds two transforms, spawns two
effects, selects an effect origin from object state, and plays two randomized
sounds. It then clears a flag and clamps a limit across six object slots before
choosing a final duration from another state bit.

## Status

Behavior recovered, not byte-matching. IDO 5.3 `-O2` emits the exact 1204-byte
length and differs in only six instruction words, starting at byte `0x247`.
They are two repeated stack spill/load orderings around the sound calls. Three
bounded stack-layout variants regressed the match, so assembly remains active.
