# func_8006D150

## Verified facts

- The USA body spans VRAM `0x8006D150` through `0x8006D7EF`, ROM `0x6DD50`,
  1696 bytes. `jr ra` is at `0x8006D7E8` with a nop delay slot;
  `func_8006D7F0` begins next.
- Its only direct caller is `func_8006E1D4`. Its path sampling, route
  correction, vector, random-number, and length helpers and tuning globals are
  recorded in the matching unit.
- Japan and Europe equivalents begin at `0x8006DCC0` and `0x80072630`.

## Behavior

The routine updates an automated steering correction. It selects a response
gain, clamps the forward sample distance, chooses a small mode-dependent state,
samples two points along the current path, and adjusts the sample distance from
their separation. It projects the vehicle offset onto its orientation and
writes a signed steering value, reducing the gain near extreme angles.

## Status

The current USA IDO 5.3 `-O2` body has the canonical 1696-byte length and
three differing instruction words, first at byte `0x14D`. At `0x8006D29C`,
the two operands of a symmetric BNE are reversed. At `0x8006D618` and
`0x8006D61C`, the Z factors of a dot product load into exchanged floating
registers before the same multiply instruction. The source remains nonexact
and assembly-backed in the USA rebuild.

Three private operand-spelling probes reverse the mode/constant comparison,
reverse the final dot-product factors, and combine both changes. All three
reproduce every baseline text byte; none is retained. The linked text hash
remains `ab2a84ae0518ff02b324b1e5dd4c10783e7cb292c31bd1061c1a2195ba6bc9bb`.
Earlier clamp-lifetime variants also failed; the direct operand-spelling
approach is closed without an exact match.
