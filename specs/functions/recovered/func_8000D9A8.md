# `func_8000D9A8` USA boundary and compiler audit

The sole observed direct caller targets `0x8000D9A8`. The two instructions at
that address load `D_8009B7F4` into `v0` and fall through directly to the body
at `0x8000D9B0`; no caller to the latter label was found. The true body ends
with the `jr ra` delay-slot store at `0x8000DA68`. Thus the two generated
assembly labels are one 196-byte callable routine, not separate C functions.

The configured combined C source is behavior-recovered but compiles to 180
bytes, with 33 differing comparable words, four missing words, and first byte
difference `0x01`. Its compiler puts the stack prologue at the entry, whereas
the ROM loads `v0` before allocating its frame at `0x8000D9B0`. Preserve the
combined boundary and investigate that prologue placement and the ROM's
register-only delay loop before attempting source-spelling variants.
