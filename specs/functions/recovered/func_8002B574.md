# func_8002B574

## Verified facts

- The USA body spans VRAM `0x8002B574` through `0x8002BBA3`, ROM `0x2C174`,
  1584 bytes. `jr ra` is at `0x8002BB9C` with a nop delay slot;
  `func_8002BBA4` begins next.
- Its direct caller is `func_8004A880`. Its formatted-text, marker visibility,
  placement, color, and text-drawing helpers and globals are in the matching
  unit.
- The Japan equivalent begins at `0x8002BFB8`. No unique Europe mapping was
  established, so Europe is not used as evidence for this recovery.

## Behavior

The routine selects a small set of context-sensitive interface hints, fades
the current hint in and out, and cycles to the next available entry. It enables
and positions the corresponding marker sprites, applies the current alpha, and
formats and draws the accompanying text at the adjusted screen position.

## Status

Behavior recovered from the USA binary and independently expressed in C; not
byte-matching. IDO 5.3 `-O2` emits the exact 1584-byte length and first differs
at byte `0x3`; 155 instruction words differ across 68 blocks. Japan was used
only as structural and relocation evidence.
