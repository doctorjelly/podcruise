# func_800105DC

## Verified facts

- The USA body spans VRAM `0x800105DC` through `0x80010B33`, ROM `0x111DC`,
  1368 bytes. `jr ra` is at `0x80010B2C`; its delay slot restores the
  216-byte stack frame. `func_80010B34` begins next.
- Its direct caller is `func_80011890`. The projection, depth-test, marker, and
  object helpers and globals are recorded in its matching unit.
- The Japan equivalent begins at `0x800105DC`; the Europe equivalent begins at
  `0x80010A6C`.

## Behavior

The routine first disables the previous ten paired interface markers. It then
examines up to 40 world points, projects points that are visible and in range,
performs a depth test, and configures at most ten marker pairs with screen
position, radius, brightness, and alpha.

## Status

Behavior recovered from the USA binary and independently expressed in C; not
byte-matching. IDO 5.3 `-O2` emits the exact 1368-byte body and first differs at
byte `0xD`; 49 instruction words differ across 29 blocks. Regional binaries
were used only as structural and relocation evidence.
