# func_8006B568

## Verified facts

- The canonical USA body spans VRAM `0x8006B568` through `0x8006B9C7`, ROM
  `0x6C168`, 1120 bytes. It returns with `jr ra` at `0x8006B9C0`; the delay
  slot at `0x8006B9C4` is a nop, and `func_8006B9C8` begins next.
- No direct USA `jal` targets this entry. The boundary has a normal 80-byte
  frame and is also supported by an aligned code pointer, suggesting indirect
  dispatch. This caller mechanism remains an inference.
- The body calls `func_80005B1C`, `func_80005B44`, `func_800155C0`,
  `func_80033328`, `func_80065CD0`, `func_80065E18`, `func_8006907C`,
  `func_8006B304`, `func_8006C708`, and `func_80081700`. It accesses
  `D_800A5B5C`, `D_800AD5E4`, `D_8011C850`, and `D_80120BF0`.
- The normalized Japan and Europe equivalents begin at `0x8006C0D8` and
  `0x80070A48`; they are structural evidence only.

## Behavior

The routine updates a moving object's transform and velocity state. It selects
attached or detached processing, applies damping and steering forces, moves a
detached target toward its limit, computes the resulting position, copies it
to the object's current and cached fields, sets detached-state flags, and
restores two temporary resource handles before returning.

## Status

Behavior recovered, not byte-matching. IDO 5.3 `-O2` emits 1128 bytes against
the 1120-byte target and first differs at byte `0x3`: the generated frame is
88 bytes instead of 80. The USA build therefore retains assembly.
