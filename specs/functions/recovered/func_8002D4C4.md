emphasis. Kind `0x4B` selects scale `0.5` with emphasis, kind `0x4D` selects
scale `0.25`, and kind `0x4F` enables emphasis without changing the scale.

`0.75`. All other kinds retain the defaults. Submit the resulting kind,
constant mode `6`, scale, intensity, and emphasis flag to the shared helper.

Its nine ordinary entries all target the default path; the four distinguished
