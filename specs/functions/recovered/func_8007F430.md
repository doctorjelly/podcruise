# func_8007F430

## Verified facts

- The USA body spans VRAM `0x8007F430` through `0x8007F55F`, ROM `0x80030`,
  304 bytes. `jr ra` is at `0x8007F558`; its delay slot is `nop`.
- `func_800801B8` installs it as a callback. It uses the cursor, matrix,
  distance, object-item, and track-position helpers recorded in its matching
  unit and selects an object from `D_8011DCF8`.
- Japan and Europe equivalents begin at `0x800800D0` and `0x80084910`.

## Behavior

Initialize a temporary track cursor, derive a scaled object index from the
track position, and reject missing, disabled, or valueless objects. For a valid
object, seek the cursor to its value, derive a point half a basis vector behind
the track transform, advance the cursor to that point, and measure the resulting
translation displacement.

## Status

Behavior recovered from the USA binary and independently expressed in C; not
byte-matching. IDO 5.3 `-O2` emits the target length of 304 bytes but first
differs at byte `0x1F`; exact-length output is not an exact match.
