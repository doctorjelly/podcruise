transform. The routine classifies local, remote, and AI tags; copies transforms
and bounds; adjusts height with a ray probe; clears motion, effects, counters,
and history buffers; selects a runtime mode; publishes attached scene nodes;
and derives display bounds and flags needed by later updates.
