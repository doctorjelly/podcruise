# func_80038E58

## Boundary

- USA VRAM range: 0x80038E58 .. 0x80038ECF inclusive (0x78 = 120 bytes, 30 words).
- ROM offset 0x39A58. Entry follows the end of func_80038DF8 and is the target
  of the one direct call site.
- The body ends with a return whose delay slot holds the last of the fifteen
  byte stores.
- The assignment's size (120) was confirmed correct; no boundary correction.

## Callers

One direct call site: func_80086A20 at 0x80086B64.

## Callees

None. The function is a leaf and needs no stack frame.

## Globals and access widths

| symbol | width | direction |
| --- | --- | --- |
| D_800A3DB0 | fifteen individual 8-bit stores at byte offsets 0x00-0x02, 0x04-0x06, 0x08-0x0A, 0x0C-0x0E and 0x10-0x12 | written |

The gaps at 0x03, 0x07, 0x0B and 0x0F are never touched, so the block is a pair
of records: an eight-byte record holding two three-byte colour triples with a
pad byte after each, followed by a twelve-byte record holding two more colour
triples and a three-byte direction, again each padded to four bytes.

## Parameters

Three pointers to 16-bit values. The first two are read as three consecutive
16-bit colour components each and only their low bytes reach memory; the third
is read as three consecutive signed 16-bit direction components, each negated
before its low byte is stored.

Both regional bodies were read independently and match the USA shape exactly,
differing only in the address of the destination block.

## Recovered behavior

The routine fills in a small fixed lighting description held at D_800A3DB0.

- The three colour components addressed by the first parameter are each written
  to two places in the first record: the primary triple and the copy triple that
  follows it. Only the low byte of each component is kept.
- The three colour components addressed by the second parameter are written the
  same way into the second record.
- The three direction components addressed by the third parameter are negated
  and their low bytes are stored into the direction triple of the second record.

Nothing is returned.

## Source shape notes

Two details were needed for byte equality. Each colour component is written with
a single chained assignment, in which the primary triple is assigned first and
the copy triple takes the resulting value: assigning them in the opposite order
swaps the two stores. Writing the two stores as separate statements instead of a
chain makes the compiler reload the source value for each store and widens the
loads, producing a longer function.

## Status

Exact on us, jp, eu and lrg_rev1.
