# func_800401FC

## Verified facts

- The USA body spans VRAM `0x800401FC` through `0x800408CF`, ROM `0x40DFC`,
  1748 bytes. `jr ra` is at `0x800408C8`; its delay slot restores the
  144-byte stack frame. `func_800408D0` begins next.
- Its only direct caller is `func_80040CE8`. Its normalization, random-number,
  renderer-update, effect-toggle helpers, global time step, and local constant
  pool are recorded in the matching unit.
- Japan and Europe equivalents begin at `0x80040B8C` and `0x80044B9C`.

## Behavior

The routine interprets an object's update flags. It resets or derives direction
and color vectors, blends saved state during a timed transition, optionally
adds or removes a randomized directional effect, and submits changed vectors
to the renderer. An invalid context index returns without modifying the object.

## Status

Behavior recovered from the USA binary and independently expressed in C; not
byte-matching. With its constant pool placed at the original address, IDO 5.3
`-O2` emits 1736 bytes for the 1748-byte target and first differs at byte
`0x13`; 384 instruction words differ across 26 blocks. Two evidence-driven
stack-layout/register-lifetime variants did not improve the match.
