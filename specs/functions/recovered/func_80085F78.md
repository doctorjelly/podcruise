# func_80085F78

## Boundary

- USA VRAM `0x80085F78` .. `0x80085FAC` inclusive, ROM `0x86B78`, size 56 bytes
  (14 instructions).
- Terminates with `jr $ra` whose delay slot stores the computed result into the
  single argument's record.
- The label is reached only by `jal`; the preceding function returns before it.
  The assigned boundary is correct.

## Callers

Two direct call sites in the USA build.

## Callees

None; no frame is set up.

## Globals

None. Only the record addressed by the single pointer argument is touched.

## Interface

One pointer to a record. Four 32-bit floating point fields are used, at byte
offsets `0x134`, `0x148`, `0x150` and `0x154`. The field at `0x154` is written;
the other three are read. Nothing is returned.

## Recovered behavior

The routine recomputes a derived scale factor for a view record.

It divides the constant 45 by the field at `0x134`, giving a ratio. One is
subtracted from that ratio to get its deviation from unity; the deviation is
scaled by the weight held at `0x150`, and one is added back, producing a blend
factor that is exactly one when the ratio is one. The base value at `0x148` is
multiplied by that blend factor and the product is stored at `0x154`.

Both constants are whole numbers whose single-precision encodings have zero low
halves, which is why the original materialises them inline rather than loading
them from a constant pool.

## Regional addresses

| version    | entry        | ROM       | size |
|------------|--------------|-----------|------|
| USA        | `0x80085F78` | `0x86B78` | 56   |
| Japan      | `0x80086C6C` | `0x8786C` | 56   |
| Europe     | `0x8008B458` | `0x8C058` | 56   |
| lrg_rev1   | `0x80085F78` | `0x86B78` | 56   |

All three other bodies were read independently and are word for word identical
to the USA body. No symbol is referenced, so only the entry name was remapped.

## Status

Exact in all four versions. One experiment was needed: giving the division its
own named local. Written as a single nested expression the arithmetic is
identical but the floating point temporaries are allocated one register pair
lower, so the constant and the quotient swap registers.
