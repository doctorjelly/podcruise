## Recovered behavior

Initialises the first 20 bytes of a dispatch record. Given the record and three
further values, it clears the leading 32-bit field to zero, stores the two
pointer arguments into the next two 32-bit fields, clears the two 16-bit
counters that follow them, and stores the fourth argument into the 32-bit field
at offset 16.

The two pointer arguments are consistently the addresses of a pair of functions
at the call sites, so the record is a handler pair plus a small identifier; the
callers supply identifiers 0 through 7. The two cleared 16-bit fields sit
between the handler pair and the identifier and are written separately, which is
what distinguishes them from a single 32-bit field.
