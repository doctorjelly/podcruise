# func_80011838

## Verified facts

- The USA body spans VRAM `0x80011838` through `0x80011887`, ROM `0x12438`,
  and is 80 bytes. Its `jr $ra` is at `0x80011880`; the delay slot at
  `0x80011884` is a `nop`. The next function begins at `0x80011888`.
- Three USA routines call it directly: `func_80052E44`, `func_80060880`, and
  `func_800865E8`. It calls no functions.
- It writes twenty four-word records at `D_800D6140` and twenty four-byte
  records at `D_800D68C0`. The byte-array endpoint is `D_800D6910`.
- Japan has the same body at VRAM/ROM `0x80011838`/`0x12438`, with relocated
  globals. Europe has it at `0x80011CC8`/`0x128C8`. Both regional instruction
  streams match the USA structure and delay slot.

## Behavioral specification

Initialize twenty parallel records. For every record, write signed minus one
to each of its four 32-bit entries and clear each of its four byte entries.

## Inference

The two arrays appear to hold parallel per-record state because the routine
advances them together, but their higher-level purpose is not yet established.
The address-based names therefore remain unchanged.

## Status

Byte-matching C for canonical USA. Exact isolated compiler comparisons against
the 80 USA bytes pass with both local IDO 5.3 and IDO 7.1 when loop unrolling is
disabled. LRG rev1 also compares exactly. Japan and Europe were used only as
structural relocation evidence and are not claimed as integrated C matches.
