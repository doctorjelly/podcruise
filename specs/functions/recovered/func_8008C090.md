# func_8008C090

## Boundary
VRAM 0x8008C090-0x8008C0FB (108 bytes). Ends with `jr $ra` and an empty delay
slot. 3 callers reach the entry; boundary confirmed.

## Callers / callees
3 callers. Calls func_8008CA80 (acquire) and func_8008CAA0 (release).

## Globals
D_800A7F54: a pointer to a record. Two full words are written at offsets 0x10
and 0x14; a halfword is written at offset 0x02.

## Regional addresses
- jp 0x8008CD80 (rom 0x8D980); global D_800AADA4; callees func_8008D770 /
  func_8008D790.
- eu 0x800915E0 (rom 0x921E0); global D_800AD254; callees func_80091FD0 /
  func_80091FF0.
- lrg_rev1 shares the USA addresses.

## Behavior
The routine takes three values. It first acquires a context from the acquire
helper and keeps that context in a register variable for the duration. It then
publishes the three values into the shared record: the first two as whole words
at the record's two word fields, the third truncated to a halfword at the
record's second halfword. Finally it hands the retained context back to the
release helper. Nothing is returned.

## Compiler note
This translation unit is built at -O1 (no common subexpression elimination of
the global pointer, no narrowing of the mask), and the retained context is a
`register` local, which is what places it in a callee-saved register and adds
its save/restore to the frame.

## Status
exact in USA, Japan, Europe and lrg_rev1 at -O1.
