# func_8006A49C

## Verified facts

- The USA body spans VRAM `0x8006A49C` through `0x8006A9D3`, ROM `0x6B09C`,
  1336 bytes. `jr ra` is at `0x8006A9CC`; its delay slot returns the computed
  floating-point result. `func_8006A9D4` begins next.
- Its direct caller is `func_8006C0D4`. The terrain/contact, collision,
  interpolation, and vector helpers and tuning globals are in its matching
  unit.
- Japan and Europe equivalents begin at `0x8006B00C` and `0x8006F97C`.

## Behavior

The routine resolves a racer's contact point and surface normal for the current
state. It chooses special or ordinary terrain handling, updates contact flags,
applies collision and height corrections, blends a transition against an
animated reference, and updates both the racer's stored result and several
part parameters before returning the resolved distance.

## Status

Behavior recovered from the USA binary and independently expressed in C; not
byte-matching. IDO 5.3 `-O2` emits the exact 1336-byte length and first differs
at byte `0x3`; 138 instruction words differ across 58 blocks. Regional
binaries were used only for structural and relocation confirmation.
