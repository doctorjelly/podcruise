# func_80024070

## Verified facts

- The USA body spans VRAM `0x80024070` through `0x80024703`, ROM `0x24C70`,
  1684 bytes. `jr ra` is at `0x800246FC`; its delay slot restores the
  376-byte stack frame. `func_80024704` begins next.
- Its only direct caller is `func_8004A880`. Its state, input, interface,
  object-animation, selection, text, and rendering helpers and globals are
  recorded in the matching unit.
- The Japan equivalent begins at `0x800247AC`. No unique Europe mapping was
  established, so Europe is not used as evidence here.

## Behavior

The routine advances a menu-like state machine. On entry to a new state it
resets shared animation values, selects the current style and text resources,
and prepares the associated objects. It then handles controller repeat and
selection changes, updates positions and transitions, draws the current text
and indicators, and may restart setup after moving to another selection.

## Status

Behavior recovered from the USA binary and independently expressed in C; not
byte-matching. IDO 5.3 `-O2` emits the exact 1684-byte length and first differs
at byte `0x19`; 290 instruction words differ across 55 blocks. Japan was used
only for structural and relocation confirmation.
