# func_80049C14

## Verified facts

- The USA body spans VRAM `0x80049C14` through `0x8004A36B`, ROM `0x4A814`,
  1880 bytes. `jr ra` is at `0x8004A364`, followed by a nop delay slot;
  `func_8004A36C` begins next.
- Its direct caller is `func_80025148`. The body uses the matrix/vector helpers
  from `func_80015268` through `func_80017BA8`, object helpers
  `func_800181BC/func_80083D80`, and the globals named in its matching unit.
- JP and EU equivalents begin at `0x8004A84C` and `0x8004E734`.

## Behavior

The routine locates an indexed effect object, builds and scales its transform,
and chooses per-index scale and translation adjustments. It refreshes shared
reference points when the selected index changes, applies the final transform,
and submits an object command when the object remains valid.
