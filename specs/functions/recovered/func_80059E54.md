# func_80059E54

## Verified facts

- The canonical USA body begins at VRAM `0x80059E54`, ROM `0x5AA54`, and
  ends after the `jr ra` delay slot at `0x8005B2CC`. Its size is `0x147C`
  (5,244) bytes; `func_8005B2D0` begins immediately afterward. The delay slot
  restores the `0x90`-byte stack frame.
- The only direct USA caller is `func_8005B3F0`. It passes the race session,
  one racer record, and that racer's object after testing the object's
  `0x02000000` flag. The third argument is saved by the callee but never read.
- The routine reads session fields at `0x0C`, `0x1BC`, `0x1C8`, `0x1D0`, and
  `0x1D4`; racer fields at `0x5C`, `0x60..0x70`, and `0x74`; and the active
  racer pointer `D_8011B1BC`. Those layouts agree with the recovered caller
  and neighboring race-HUD routines.
- All USA branches and delay slots were reviewed in three address windows.
  There are no indirect jumps or jump tables. The initial branch-likely loops,
  floating-point conversions, stack-passed color/text arguments, early-return
  paths, and terminal stack-restoring delay slot were checked directly.
- The body makes 37 static calls. The IDO 5.3 candidate emits the same 37
  targets in the same order: sprite visibility/placement, text formatting,
  text/time drawing, random color selection, and one clipping rectangle.
- By adjacency and matching entry behavior, the regional structural versions
  are JP `func_8005A9D4` (`0x14A8` bytes) and EU `func_8005EF18` (`0x1800`
  bytes). Localization and regional rendering differences prevent a unique
  automatic mapping, so they were used only as a structural spot check.

## Behavioral specification

Render the completed-race result rows and the moving placement marker for one
racer. The routine first hides the sprite IDs belonging to the active player
layout, chooses the vertical origin and the horizontal column needed for the
racer's displayed time, then draws each recorded split with its label and a
highlight color when it beats the session threshold.

It next draws the racer's final time, updates the session's best value when the
new value is no greater, and selects either randomized or fixed diagnostic
colors. In multiplayer layout it formats the racer's placement and draws the
corresponding label pair. In the single-layout path it moves one of three
placement sprites across the screen during the session's eight-second result
animation, clips and positions that sprite, and draws its text pair. Once the
animation passes eight seconds, all three placement sprites are hidden.

## Recovery notes

- The row pointer advances four bytes because the five values at racer offsets
  `0x60..0x70` are a float array; the loop index is a separate integer.
- Halfword loads from stack offsets `0x42`, `0x7E`, `0x8A`, and `0x8E` are the
  low halves of stored screen coordinates, not independent data fields.
  Specifically, `sp+0x42` is copied from the selected value column at
  `sp+0x88`; it is not derived from the unused third argument at `sp+0x98`.
- The racer selector remains live in `a2` at three `func_8000A920` call sites,
  but that callee reads only `a0` and `a1`; the recovered two-argument calls
  reflect the verified callee contract.
- The long `cfc1`/`ctc1` sequences implement unsigned float-to-integer
  conversion before masking colors to eight bits. The recovered C uses the
  corresponding `u32` then `u8` conversions and preserves every random-number
  call site.

## Status

**Behavior-recovered C, not byte-matching.** With the recorded IDO 5.3 `-O2`
profile, the candidate is `0x1374` (4,980) bytes versus the canonical `0x147C`
(5,244), and first differs at byte `0x3`. Equal call count/order and the
reviewed control flow support the behavioral recovery, but the USA split must
continue using assembly until an exact compiler comparison succeeds.
