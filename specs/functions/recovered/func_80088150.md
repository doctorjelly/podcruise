# func_80088150

## Boundary

- USA VRAM range: 0x80088150 .. 0x800882AC inclusive (0x160 = 352 bytes, 88 instructions).
- USA ROM offset: 0x88D50.
- Ends with a return whose delay slot releases a sixteen-byte frame. The frame
  is reserved for three register-class locals even though none of them is ever
  spilled; there are no calls, so the routine is a leaf.
- Two direct call sites, both targeting 0x80088150. Assigned size 352 was
  correct; no boundary correction.

## Callers and callees

- Two direct callers. One unsigned 32-bit argument, one signed 32-bit result.
- Leaf function; no callees.

## Globals and access widths

- `D_800A7B48` — one signed 32-bit word, read twice: once as the numerator of
  the floating-point ratio and once again as the numerator of the final integer
  division.
- Three memory-mapped hardware words at 0xA4500010, 0xA4500014 and 0xA4500008.
  They are addressed as plain constants, not as linked data symbols: the upper
  half is materialised into an ordinary temporary register and the low half
  rides in the store's displacement, which is what a volatile pointer built
  from a literal address produces. Treating them as external symbols instead
  routes the stores through the assembler's absolute-store macro and its
  reserved temporary, which does not match.

## Regional addresses

The body is instruction-for-instruction identical in every version; only the
function's own address and the clock word move.

## Recovered behavior

Programs the audio hardware for a requested sample rate and reports the rate
actually achieved.

The caller passes a desired frequency as an unsigned value. The routine divides
the global clock word by it in single precision, rounds the quotient to nearest
by adding a half and truncating toward zero, and treats the result as an
unsigned divider. A divider below one hundred and thirty-two is rejected: the
requested frequency is too high for the hardware and the routine returns minus
one without touching any register.

Otherwise it derives a bit-rate figure by dividing the divider by sixty-six,
keeping only the low eight bits, and clamping it to a maximum of sixteen. It
then writes the divider minus one to the sample-rate register, the bit rate
minus one to the bit-rate register, and a one to the control register to enable
the interface. Finally it re-reads the clock word and returns it divided by the
divider as a signed quotient — the true frequency the hardware will produce.

Source-shape notes established by matching:

- This translation unit was not built at the project's usual optimisation
  level. At `-O2` the compiler folds away the frame, drops the dead condition
  mask that the conversion sequence emits, and turns two of the branches into
  their likely forms; the result is twenty-four bytes short. At `-O1`, with the
  rest of the flag list unchanged, all of that reappears exactly.
- At `-O1` the three locals must be declared with the register storage class,
  otherwise the divider and bit rate live in stack slots and every use becomes a
  reload. Declaring all three (including the intermediate ratio) is also what
  produces the sixteen-byte frame, since the compiler still reserves the space.
- The rounding must be written as a single expression that stores the ratio plus
  a half into the floating-point local and converts that local, not as a ratio
  local converted with the half added at the conversion site; the two forms
  allocate the two floating-point registers the other way round.

## Status

Exact under a per-unit `-O1` profile. Byte-identical in USA, Japan, Europe and
the large-cartridge revision.
