# func_80096158

## Verified facts

- The callable USA entry is VRAM `0x80096158`, ROM `0x96D58`. The scanner's
  `func_8009615C` label is a false split after the entry's first instruction:
  the later body immediately consumes the value placed in `v1` at
  `0x80096158`. The merged body is 604 bytes and ends with `jr $ra` at
  `0x800963AC` and a `nop` delay slot at `0x800963B0`.
- `func_8008D870` stores `func_80096158` as a callback. It operates on the
  40-byte voice records allocated there through the pointer at sequence offset
  `0x20`.
- The second argument selects a voice and one of eight fields after subtracting
  two. The third argument points to the signed word being applied.
- Seven cases update fields at offsets `0x00`, `0x04`, `0x08`, `0x0A`, `0x0C`,
  `0x10`, and `0x1C`. The eighth updates the halfword reached through the
  pointer at `0x20` and calls `func_8008D7D0`.
- The jump table is at `0x800AE140`; its entries prove the nonsequential case-2
  and case-3 field order. The adjacent double constant is also used by the
  existing voice initializer in `func_8008D870`.
- Japan (`0x80096E48`, ROM `0x97A48`) and Europe (`0x8009B6A8`, ROM
  `0x9C2A8`) have the same merged 604-byte boundary, including the prefix and
  final return/delay-slot sequence.

## Behavior

Decode the selector into a voice index and one of eight update commands. Store
masked range endpoints, three signed halfwords, or one of two derived float
rates in the selected voice. For the final command, update its attached channel
and notify the channel helper when present. Every command returns zero.

## Status

Behavior recovered from the canonical USA binary and independently expressed
in C. IDO 5.3 `-O2` emits 572 bytes for the 604-byte target and first differs
at byte `0x4`; this is not claimed as byte-matching.
